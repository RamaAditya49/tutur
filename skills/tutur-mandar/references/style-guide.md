# Style Guide

## Language Identity

- Name: Bahasa Mandar. ISO 639-3 `mdr`. Glottocode `mand1442`.
- Home area for this skill: Sulawesi Barat, especially Polewali Mandar and Majene.
- Not Bugis. Not Makassar. Not Mamasa. Not Mamuju.
- Dictionary varieties to keep apart when the user names them: `basa to Balanipa`, `basa to Banggae`, `basa to Pamboang`.
- Spelling: glottal as `q`, following the 2025 Balai Bahasa dictionary.

## Safety Rules

- Do not invent vocabulary.
- Do not import `tabe'` or `iye`. The Mandar form that is actually glossed permisi is `tabeq`.
- Do not build new sentences from loose roots.
- Keep policy, billing, medical, school, and government details in Indonesian.
- Ask for the kabupaten when the note will be posted in public.

## Registers

### Everyday

`Meloaq ummande`, `poleo medondong`, `palaimoqo`, `malaiaq mendiolo`. These already contain the person. Adding `iyau` on the front is the emphatic pattern, as in `iyau pole dionging`, not the default.

### Public

`Tabeq` can open a neighborhood note. The time and the place stay Indonesian. Say that the Mandar line is from the 2025 dictionary, not a local proclamation.

### Professional

Admin messages use Indonesian for the request. A Mandar sentence is allowed when it is one of the printed examples and matches the act, for example `poleo medondong` if the ask is really "come tomorrow".

### Adat

Do not compose ritual speech or `kalindaqdaq`. The dictionary records the name of that back-and-forth verse. New verses belong to people who perform them.
