# Sources

Language: Mandar

Region/context: Sulawesi Barat, especially Polewali Mandar and Majene, with further Peta Bahasa points in Pinrang and Masalembu. Not Bugis. Not Makassar.

## Source Confidence

Level 2. Kamus Mandar–Indonesia edisi revisi (2025) is an official Balai Bahasa dictionary and supports the short examples in this skill. Full public translation still needs a Mandar speaker. Variety labels inside the dictionary (`basa to Balanipa`, `basa to Banggae`, `basa to Pamboang`) are not interchangeable.

## Editorial Position

Ordinary chat may use the printed dictionary examples, with operational detail in Indonesian. The front matter of the dictionary says the glottal phoneme is written `q` for ease of reading. Follow that spelling. Do not import `tabe'` or `iye` from Bugis or Makassar skills. `tabeq` is available only because the Mandar dictionary glosses it as permisi.

Do not copy further dictionary pages into a message. Do not compose `kalindaqdaq`. The dictionary names that verse exchange; it does not supply a model poem for this skill to extend.
