# Tutur Mandar

## Overview

Use this skill for ordinary Bahasa Mandar. Peta Bahasa places the language in Polewali Mandar and Majene, Sulawesi Barat, and also records pockets in Pinrang and one point in Masalembu, Sumenep. It reports an 81–100 percent difference from Mamasa and Mamuju. The phrases here follow Kamus Mandar–Indonesia, edisi revisi, Balai Bahasa Provinsi Sulawesi Selatan, Kementerian Pendidikan Dasar dan Menengah, 2025.

Catalog identity: ISO 639-3 `mdr`, glottocode `mand1442`. The dictionary writes the glottal stop as `q`, not as an apostrophe. Free pronouns such as `iyau` and `iqo` are for emphasis. Ordinary sentences in the dictionary's own examples hang the person on the verb: `meloaq ummande`, `peqoro moqo`. Respectful second person is `itaq`, not `iqo`.

The same dictionary names `basa to Balanipa`, `basa to Banggae`, and `basa to Pamboang` as distinct. Do not flatten them. Bugis and Makassar are different languages. `tabeq` "permisi" is used here only because this Mandar dictionary glosses it. Do not bring in `iye`.

Source confidence: Level 2. The 2025 dictionary supports short examples. It is not a license to translate a whole official letter.

## Human Voice

Di kamus, orang tidak wajib menaruh `iyau` atau `iqo` di depan. Pelaku menempel: `meloaq ummande` kalau saya mau makan, `peqoro moqo` kalau menyuruh duduk. Hamzah ditulis `q`, jadi permisi di kamus ini `tabeq`, bukan salinan `tabe'` dari bahasa lain. Orang yang dihormati dipanggil dengan `itaq`, bukan `iqo`. Kabar yang sudah ada glossnya: `diang karewa macoa u tarima`. Sisanya bahasa Indonesia.

## Workflow

1. Identify the task.
   - Ask which area if the text will be public: Polewali Mandar, Majene, Balanipa, Banggae, Pamboang, or a diaspora chat.
   - Do not assume one variety covers the province.

2. Check source confidence.
   - Use `references/sources.md` and the short list in `references/local-mirror.md`.
   - Keep dictionary `q`. Do not respell it with an apostrophe.

3. Compose with source discipline.
   - Prefer the clitic example over a free pronoun when the dictionary already gives that sentence.
   - Keep names, dates, money, and places unchanged.
   - Label mixed text as mixed.

4. Final pass.
   - Remove any `iye` or apostrophe-`tabe'` that came from a Bugis or Makassar habit.
   - Leave unlisted words in Indonesian.

## Output Style

- Direct request: the message first.
- Review request: the unsupported words, then a shorter rewrite.
- Admin request: Indonesian for anything that must be acted on.
