# Offline Brief

## Local Facts

- Purpose: ordinary messages, short greetings, and review for Bahasa Mandailing.
- Region: Mandailing Natal, especially speech people contrast with Toba. Peta Bahasa also maps dialek Mandailing south of Danau Toba and on the Sumbar, Riau, and Aceh edges.
- ISO 639-3: `btm`. Glottocode: `bata1291`. Glottolog name: Batak Mandailing, inside Angkola-Mandailing.
- Peta Bahasa does not give Mandailing its own language page. It lists dialek Mandailing under Bahasa Batak, beside Toba, Simalungun, Pakpak, and Karo. This skill still keeps Mandailing off the Toba register.
- Source confidence: Level 2. The 2021 Balai Bahasa dictionary is joint Angkola-Mandailing.
- Speech habit: open with santabi, then the errand. Call a younger sibling anggi when that is the relationship in the sentence. Do not use horas as a Toba-style hello.
- The dictionary glosses horas as selamat. Its example is "sai horas hita ibaen Tuhan", glossed "kita tetap selamat dibuat Tuhan".
- Attested lines to reuse are copied in `local-mirror.md`. They are not a license to translate a paragraph.
- Akl in the dictionary means Angkola. Mdl means Mandailing. Unlabeled entries are shared and may be used, with that limit stated.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent vocabulary, kinship titles, or ritual lines.
- Do not treat an Akl label as Mandailing.
- Do not write horja, markobar, or wedding speeches from a dictionary gloss.
- Keep Indonesian when the Mandailing word is missing.
- Do not answer with Batak Toba, Karo, Simalungun, or Pakpak.
- Do not assume one Mandailing village spelling covers Panyabungan, Kotanopan, and the Pasaman border.
- Ask for a local sample when the user names a desa.

## Safe Output Patterns

- For a group, quote "santabi di hamu sude", then give the errand in Indonesian.
- For one person, the word santabi may open the note. Say the full dictionary sentence is the group formula. Mark the note mixed.
- Use mauliate only as the glossed thanks noun, not as a new sentence frame.
- For review, flag Toba horas-as-hello and Akl-only words.
- For unknown terms, keep Indonesian and name the gap.
- For public use, ask a Mandailing speaker.
- For admin facts, keep time, price, and document names in Indonesian.
- Mark mixed lines as mixed.
