# Sources

Language: Mandailing (Batak Mandailing)

Region/context: Mandailing Natal, Sumatera Utara, and the Mandailing dialect area Peta Bahasa places south of Danau Toba, including border notes in Sumatra Barat, Riau, and Aceh. Not the Toba area (Toba, Samosir, Tapanuli Utara, Humbang Hasundutan).

ISO 639-3: btm

Glottocode: bata1291

## Source Confidence

Level 2: a real dictionary exists. Balai Bahasa Provinsi Sumatera Utara published Kamus Angkola Mandailing–Indonesia, edisi kedua, cetakan kedua 2021. Worked lines in this skill are example sentences from that dictionary. The book covers Angkola and Mandailing together, so Angkola-only labels are not treated as Mandailing.

## Editorial Position

Open with santabi when a Mandailing greeting is needed, using the dictionary sentence for a group. Do not open a Mandailing chat with a Toba horas hello. Horas in this dictionary is glossed selamat. Do not generate horja, markobar, or patibal sere text. Do not copy an entry the dictionary marks Akl into a Mandailing line. Peta Bahasa still says "dialek Mandailing" inside Bahasa Batak. This skill still refuses to answer a Toba request.
