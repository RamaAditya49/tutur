# Tutur Mandailing

## Overview

Use this skill for Bahasa Mandailing in Mandailing Natal and in Mandailing speech along the southern edge of Danau Toba, including notes in Pasaman and Rokan Hulu where Peta Bahasa still files the same isolek as dialek Mandailing of Bahasa Batak. Glottolog lists it separately as Batak Mandailing, ISO 639-3 `btm`, Glottocode `bata1291`, under Angkola-Mandailing, not under Batak Toba.

The working dictionary is Kamus Angkola Mandailing–Indonesia from Balai Bahasa Provinsi Sumatera Utara. Shared entries may be used. Entries labeled Akl are Angkola and stay out of a Mandailing line. Horja and markobar are not generated from a gloss.

## Search And Discovery

Relevant searches: Bahasa Mandailing, kamus Bahasa Mandailing, kamus Angkola Mandailing, contoh Bahasa Mandailing, belajar Bahasa Mandailing, terjemahan Indonesia Mandailing, translate Bahasa Mandailing, hata Mandailing, Mandailing Natal, Panyabungan, santabi, AI skill Bahasa Mandailing.

Use this skill for source-aware Mandailing generation and review. It is not a certified translator. Angkola-only forms, adat, public, legal, educational, or official text stay with a Mandailing speaker when the line is not in the local sources.

## Human Voice

Santabi dulu, baru isi pesannya. Di chat kerabat, yang lebih muda dipanggil anggi kalau memang adik, lalu urusannya langsung: jemput, jam, atau siapa yang ditunggu. Horas di kamus Balai Bahasa artinya selamat, contohnya doa singkat "sai horas hita ibaen Tuhan", bukan sapaan pembuka gaya Toba. Jangan tempel horas Toba lalu kira sudah Mandailing. Kalimat yang belum ada di kamus dibiarkan bahasa Indonesia.

## Workflow

1. Identify the task.
   - Translation needs a printed Mandailing or shared Angkola-Mandailing sample.
   - Style requests keep the Indonesian meaning and add only attested Mandailing.
   - If the user says Batak without naming Mandailing, ask. Toba, Karo, Simalungun, Pakpak, and Angkola are not this skill.

2. Check source confidence.
   - Read `references/sources.md` first.
   - Prefer the 2021 Balai Bahasa dictionary. Skip lemmas marked Akl when the request is Mandailing.
   - Peta Bahasa nests Mandailing inside Bahasa Batak. Glottolog and ISO 639-3 split Mandailing (`btm`) from Toba. Follow the split the user asked for: this folder is Mandailing, not Toba.
   - Community greeting blogs are support only. Do not let them override the dictionary.

3. Compose with source discipline.
   - Keep names, places, titles, dates, and facts unchanged.
   - Use verified Mandailing words only.
   - If the sentence is not attested, write Indonesian and label the result mixed.

4. Final pass.
   - Name the source line.
   - Do not claim native-speaker or official authority.
   - Do not answer in Batak Toba.

## Output Style

- For a direct request, give the message first.
- For a review, name the risk, then give a safer rewrite.
- For public, adat, legal, school, or government text, ask for a Mandailing speaker review.
- Label mixed Mandailing-Indonesian text as mixed.
