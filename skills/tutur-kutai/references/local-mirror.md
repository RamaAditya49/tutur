# Local Mirror

## Source Mirror

- Language/register: spoken Bahasa Kutai in the Kantor Bahasa dictionary. Default label: Tenggarong-centered.
- Region/context: Kutai Kartanegara and the Mahakam corridor, Kalimantan Timur. Not Banjar.
- Glottolog: Tenggarong Kutai Malay `teng1267` (ISO `vkt`); Kota Bangun Kutai Malay `kota1275` (ISO `mqg`).
- 2021 revision dropped some non-Kutai entries and dropped f-initial headwords. The preface says f is a borrowing from Indonesian, not a Kutai phoneme in the informants' account.
- 2013 preface: three dialects (Tenggarong, Kota Bangun, Muara Ancalong) with morphological and vocabulary differences.
- Peta Bahasa idb=123 is Bahasa Melayu Kalimantan Timur, not a single Kutai page. Kutai-named dialects sit inside that Malay entry beside Banjar Samarinda.

## Local Usability

Use this offline for Tenggarong-centered lines that the dictionary already prints as examples. A Kota Bangun sentence needs a Kota Bangun source. The kamus does not mark each example as KT, KKB, or KMA, so this skill does not pretend an example is Kota Bangun.

## Vocabulary And Semantics

Dictionary examples, 2021 revision:

- `hambat-hambat nyawa blm mendi, nya dah datang berayak` — pagi-pagi sekali aku belum mandi, dia sudah datang bertamu. Head `hambat-hambat`.
- `aku endak mendi dulu, lalu besalin` — aku mau mandi dulu, kemudian ganti pakaian. The example continues into `osok` (rasa tidak nyaman). Quote the bathing clause for that meaning only.
- `ai, napa nya macam tu` — ai, mengapa dia begitu. `ai` is the surprise word. `nya` is dia, not kamu.
- `awak tu ngeramput, tu namanya ngada-ada` — yang kau katakan itu tidak betul, kamu hanya mengada-ada.
- `endik beguna nyalaminya tu, endik tahu nyahuti` — tidak ada gunanya memberi salam kepadanya, dia tidak pernah membalas.
- `ni merian megang ni, cepat mendi, bebaju, etam pegi sujud ngan mek, bapak, sida tua` — sekarang petang menjelang hari mulai puasa, cepat mandi, berpakaian, kita pergi sujud kepada ibu, ayah, dan saudara yang lebih tua. Occasion-specific.

Pieces inside those glosses: `nyawa` aku, `awak` kamu, `etam` kita, `nya` dia, `mendi` mandi, `blm` belum, `endak` mau, `endik` tidak, `pegi` pergi, `ngan` dengan, `mek` ibu. From the 2013 usage sample, `sida` mereka: `diapakan sida ember ni` "mereka apakan ember ini".

Preface pairs, not free sentences: jalan/jelan, darah/derah, balian/belian, mandi/mendi. `mendi` is also inside the examples above.

## Generation Rules

1. Name Tenggarong-centered kamus unless the user brought a sample of another variety.
2. Refuse to relabel the same line as Kota Bangun or Banjar.
3. Put logistics in Indonesian.
4. Use `ai, napa nya macam tu` only about a third person, because `nya` is dia.
5. Keep the megang sentence on the evening before Ramadan.
6. Do not mark the result as official or native-verified.
