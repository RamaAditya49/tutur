# Style Guide

## Language Identity

- Name: Bahasa Kutai.
- Default in this skill: Tenggarong-centered Kamus Bahasa Kutai—Indonesia.
- Glottocode / ISO: Tenggarong `teng1267` / `vkt`. Kota Bangun `kota1275` / `mqg`.
- Other names to keep apart: Muara Ancalong, Muara Lesan, Muyub Ulu, Banjar Samarinda.
- Region: Mahakam, Kalimantan Timur.

## Safety

- Do not invent vocabulary or pantun.
- Do not treat f-initial Indonesian loans as native Kutai headwords. The 2021 preface removed them.
- Do not call Kutai a kind of Banjar. Peta Bahasa lists dialek Banjar Samarinda separately under Melayu Kalimantan Timur.
- Neighbors on the river (Benuaq, Tunjung, Bahau) are other languages. The 2013 preface lists them as co-residents, not as Kutai dialects.

## Everyday

Oral, short, and ready to switch into Indonesian. `nyawa`, `awak`, `etam`, and `mendi` are the habits the examples actually show. Surprise is `ai`. Disagreement can be blunt: `awak tu ngeramput`.

## Public

Notices keep the time and the meeting place in Indonesian. A dictionary sentence may sit in front only when its gloss is true of the situation. Early-morning guest talk is not a public-service slogan.

## Professional

Office facts stay Indonesian. The kamus examples on hand are household and social, not form letters. Do not dress a deadline in Kutai words that were not in an example.

## Adat Boundary

The megang example is the evening before Ramadan: bathe, dress, and `etam pegi sujud ngan mek, bapak, sida tua`. Use that sentence for that occasion only. Pantun and Jawi sultanate writing are a different layer. Do not compose either one.
