# Sources

Language: Kutai

Region/context: Mahakam, Kalimantan Timur. Default variety in this skill: Kutai Tenggarong as centered in the Kantor Bahasa dictionary. Kota Bangun, Muara Ancalong, Muara Lesan, and Muyub Ulu are named separately. Not Banjar.

## Source Confidence

Level 2: Kamus Bahasa Kutai—Bahasa Indonesia is a real dictionary (Kantor Bahasa Provinsi Kalimantan Timur, first printing August 2013, revision November 2021, ISBN 978-602-52053-7-8). Sentences below are short examples from that dictionary, not a copy of its pages. Glottolog separates Tenggarong and Kota Bangun. Peta Bahasa files several Kutai-named dialects under Bahasa Melayu Kalimantan Timur and lists Banjar Samarinda as its own dialect there.

## Editorial Position

Chat may repeat a dictionary example when the gloss is the point, and must say the line is from the Tenggarong-centered kamus. Kota Bangun needs its own sample. Banjar Samarinda is not Kutai. Pantun and Jawi court writing are named in the 2013 preface as the old written layer; they are not generated here.
