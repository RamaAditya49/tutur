# Tutur Muna

## Overview

Use this skill for ordinary Bahasa Muna. Speakers call the language `wamba Wuna`, the people `miendo Wuna`, and the island `witeno Wuna`. `Wuna` in that account means flower. Indonesian and maps say Muna. Peta Bahasa places the language on Pulau Muna and the west coast of Pulau Buton, in Kabupaten Muna, Muna Barat, Buton, Buton Utara, Buton Tengah, and Kota Bau-Bau, next to Kulisusu, Bajo, Wolio, Cia-Cia, and others. Those neighbors are not this language.

The sentences here follow René van den Berg, A Grammar of the Muna Language. He describes standard Muna, the prestige variety of Tongkuno, Kabawo, Lawa, and Katobu, and he bases the grammar on Kecamatan Katobu, which includes Raha. Southern Gu and Mawasangka are the group he calls Gumas. Tiworo, in Tikep, is another dialect. Do not treat those as Katobu wording. Peta Bahasa, quoting Burhanuddin, lists a different dialect set, including a Wuna group whose subdialects include Mawasangka. Keep that map label separate from van den Berg's standard Muna.

Catalog identity: ISO 639-3 `mnb`, glottocode `muna1247`.

Source confidence: Level 2. The grammar is a full published grammar. A 1985 Pusat Bahasa dictionary and a 2023 Kantor Bahasa Sulawesi Tenggara dictionary exist; their entries are not copied here.

## Human Voice

Di jalan, sapaan yang dicatat van den Berg bukan selamat pagi. Orang ditanya mau ke mana: `o-k[um]ala ne hamai?` Kurung siku di situ tanda sisipan, bukan huruf yang dibaca. Siang hari ada sapaan lain, `ka-ghindotu-no gholeo itu-a`, matahari sudah di puncak. Pelaku menempel di depan kata, `a-` untuk saya dan `o-` untuk kamu, seperti `a-po-guru-ghoo wamba Wuna`. Bahasa ini `wamba Wuna`, bukan Wolio dan bukan Tolaki, dan kalimat yang belum ada di tata bahasa itu tetap bahasa Indonesia.

## Workflow

1. Identify the task.
   - If the place is Raha or Katobu, the grammar examples apply as standard Muna.
   - If the place is Gu, Mawasangka, Tiworo, or a Buton village, say the examples are Katobu and do not pretend they were recorded there.
   - If the user asks for Wolio or Tolaki, stop and say this skill is Muna.

2. Check source confidence.
   - Quote grammar examples with their hyphens and brackets, and explain the brackets in a note.
   - Do not modernize the spelling into a new sentence.

3. Compose with source discipline.
   - One attested line, then Indonesian for the plan, the hour, and the place.
   - `ne` and `we` both appear as locatives in different examples. Do not swap them.
   - Label mixed text as mixed.

4. Final pass.
   - Check that no Wolio or Tolaki particle was added.
   - Leave unknown words in Indonesian.

## Output Style

- Direct request: the message first.
- Review request: the unsupported words, then a shorter rewrite.
- Admin request: Indonesian for the instruction.
