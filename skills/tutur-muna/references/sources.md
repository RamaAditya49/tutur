# Sources

Language: Muna, endonym wamba Wuna

Region/context: Pulau Muna and part of the west coast of Pulau Buton, Sulawesi Tenggara. Examples in this skill are standard Muna, Kecamatan Katobu, including Raha. Not Wolio. Not Tolaki. Not the Gumas or Tiworo dialect as described by van den Berg.

## Source Confidence

Level 2. Van den Berg's grammar is a published reference grammar and the source of the sentences here. Two dictionaries are known and were not opened for entries: M. Arief Mattalitti, Kamus Muna–Indonesia, Pusat Pembinaan dan Pengembangan Bahasa, 1985, and the Kamus Bahasa Muna–Indonesia launched by Kantor Bahasa Sulawesi Tenggara on 29 November 2023. Do not invent headwords from either book.

## Editorial Position

Chat and short reminders may use a grammar example that already means the thing being said, then Indonesian. Brackets in `k[um]ala` are van den Berg's mark for the infix `-um-`, as he states with `na-k[um]ala` "he will go". They are not a second alphabet.

Wolio is a different language. The grammar itself can talk about `wamba Wolio` as Wolio, separate from `wamba Wuna`. Tolaki is also separate. Van den Berg notes that Tobea Besar had been misassigned to Tolaki and that the Muna there is standard Muna from central Muna. That fact is about Tobea Besar, not a reason to mix Tolaki words into Muna text.

Adat formulas are not generated.
