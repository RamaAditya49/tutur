# Offline Brief

## Local Facts

- Purpose: ordinary Muna-aware chat, reminders, and review.
- Endonym from the grammar: `wamba Wuna` the language, `miendo Wuna` the people, `witeno Wuna` the island. `wuna` means flower in that account.
- Exonym: Muna, Bahasa Muna.
- ISO 639-3: `mnb`. Glottocode: `muna1247`.
- Variety of the examples: standard Muna, subdialect of Kecamatan Katobu, including Raha. Standard Muna is also the name van den Berg gives to the prestige speech of Tongkuno, Kabawo, Lawa, and Katobu.
- Other varieties he separates: Gumas (Gu and Mawasangka, southern Muna) and Tiworo (Tikep and the area around Kambara).
- Peta Bahasa quotes a five-dialect list from Burhanuddin, with Wuna, Gu, Katobengke, Kadatua, and Siompu. That list is not the same cut as standard Muna versus Gumas. Do not merge them in one sentence.
- Neighbors that are not Muna: Wolio, Tolaki, Kulisusu, Cia-Cia, Bajo.
- Level 2 for the grammar lines listed in `local-mirror.md`.
- Street habit: ask where someone is going. Noon habit: remark that the sun is high.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent vocabulary.
- Do not drop the person prefix and hope the sentence still works.
- Do not swap `ne` and `we`.
- Do not read the square brackets aloud or delete them without saying they mark an infix.
- Do not write Wolio or Tolaki forms into a Muna line.
- Do not treat a Gumas or Tiworo request as already covered by the Katobu examples.
- Keep times, money, medicine, and official instructions in Indonesian.
- Label mixed text as mixed.
- Do not compose adat or ritual formulas.

## Sample Prompts

- "Use $tutur-muna to greet someone I pass on the road in Raha."
- "Say I am learning wamba Wuna, then add the class time in Indonesian."
- "Review a text that calls Wolio words Bahasa Muna."
- "The user is in Mawasangka. Do not pretend the Katobu line was recorded there."

## Safe Output Patterns

- On the road: `o-k[um]ala ne hamai?`
- At noon: `ka-ghindotu-no gholeo itu-a!`
- Learning the language: `a-po-guru-ghoo wamba Wuna.`
- Tomorrow to town: `naewine a-k[um]ala we Raha`, then the Indonesian reason.
- If the dialect is not Katobu or unspecified standard Muna, keep the body in Indonesian.
