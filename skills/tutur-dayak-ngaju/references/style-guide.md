# Style Guide

## Language Identity

- Name: Bahasa Ngaju, Dayak Ngaju.
- Folder: tutur-dayak-ngaju.
- ISO 639-3 `nij`. Glottocode `ngaj1237`.
- Area: Kalimantan Tengah as mapped for Dayak Ngaju. Village dialects are many. Examples follow the 2013 Balai Bahasa dictionary, not one desa.
- Glottolog alternate names such as Kapuas and Kahayan are historical labels for this language. They are not other languages to mix in, and they are not a pan-Dayak category.
- Not this skill: any Dayak language that is not Ngaju.
- Speech habit to name: river direction, with ngaju for somewhat upstream, and printed code-mixing such as tarima kasih inside an Ngaju sentence.

## Safety Rules

- Do not invent vocabulary, ritual language, or kinship titles.
- Do not let Palangka Raya stand for every Ngaju dialect.
- Do not write adat, legal, or official text from a greeting line.
- Keep Indonesian when the Ngaju form is unknown.
- Ask for a sample when the user names a desa and wants local spelling.
- Do not answer "bahasa Dayak" with this folder unless the user says Ngaju.

## Practical Use

Use this skill for source-aware Ngaju lines and review of machine text. It fits chat and short community notes. It does not fit an Ngaju-only announcement or a ceremony script.

## Registers

### Everyday

Attested help line from the dictionary:

> dohop ikei

That line means "bantu kami". The task after it stays in Indonesian. Mark the note mixed.

### Public

Quote a full example only when its meaning matches the notice. A work-bee reminder is not the sit-down line and not the travel prayer. Use Indonesian for the time and place. Mark the notice mixed.

### Professional

Admin text keeps deadlines, money, and document names in Indonesian. The printed thanks sentence thanks people for pertolongan. Use it only for that meaning. Do not attach it to an invoice.

### Adat boundary

Do not write ritual, funeral, or healing formulas. The mandoa example is a wish that people arrive safely at the village. Do not extend it into a ceremony. Ask for the text the family already uses.
