# Tutur Dawan

## Overview

Use this skill for Bahasa Dawan on Timor, the language speakers in the field study call uab meto or molok meto, also called Atoni in catalog names. Peta Bahasa writes Bahasa Dawan (Timor) and lists nine dialects. The words reused from the 2025 comparison are only the Oenames and Kaesmetan subdialects in the Amfoang area. They are not a single Dawan for Soe, Kefamenanu, and Kupang.

A full Dawan sentence is not assembled from separate glosses. Tetun and Melayu Kupang are different languages.

Use this skill for source-aware Dawan generation and review. It is not a certified translator. Dialect, adat, public, legal, educational, or official text stays with a speaker of the named variety when the line is not in the local sources.

Di kampung orang bilang Uab Meto atau molok meto, sementara Peta Bahasa menulis Bahasa Dawan (Timor), dan tiap desa punya sebutan sendiri. Jangan samakan dengan Tetun atau Melayu Kupang. leko di data Amfoang artinya sehat, jadi kata itu bukan balasan terima kasih. Kalimat yang belum tercetak utuh ditulis dalam bahasa Indonesia.

## Workflow

1. Identify the task.
   - Translation needs a printed sample of the named variety.
   - Style requests keep the Indonesian meaning and add only attested Dawan.
   - Ask the variety before choosing a word. Oenames and Kaesmetan are not interchangeable even inside Amfoang.

2. Check source confidence.
   - Read `references/sources.md` first.
   - Peta Bahasa is the map. The Humanis comparison is the wordlist, and it covers two Amfoang subdialects.
   - The Wikipedia phrase table is support for Tabe, Hao, and Kaha' only. Where it disagrees with the field study, follow the field study.
   - Tata Bahasa Dawan (1992) shows that a grammar exists. This skill does not invent sentences from the book's title.

3. Compose with source discipline.
   - Keep names, places, titles, dates, and facts unchanged.
   - Use verified Dawan words only, with the variety label.
   - If the sentence is not attested, write Indonesian and label the result mixed.

4. Final pass.
   - Say the variety in the note.
   - Do not claim native-speaker or official authority.
   - Do not answer in Tetun, Baikeno Portuguese-influenced lines, or Melayu Kupang.

## Output Style

- For a direct request, give the message first.
- For a review, name the risk, then give a safer rewrite.
- For public, adat, legal, school, or government text, ask for a speaker of that valley's variety.
- Label mixed Dawan-Indonesian text as mixed.
