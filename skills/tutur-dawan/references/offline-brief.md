# Offline Brief

## Local Facts

- Purpose: ordinary messages, short words, and review for Bahasa Dawan / Uab Meto / Atoni.
- Region: Timor, Nusa Tenggara Timur. Peta Bahasa maps Bahasa Dawan (Timor) in Kabupaten Kupang, Ambenu, Timor Tengah Utara, and Timor Tengah Selatan.
- Names on that map, by village: Timor Dawan (Camplong I, Oenoni, Teunbaun), Timor Naikono (Bipolo, Hauteas, Abani, Oepliki), Timor (Tetaf, Manufui), Dewan (Sallu, Manunain), and Dawan (Netpala, Nenas, Bijeli, Nobi-Nobi, Lotas, Lilo).
- The field study's speakers call the language uab meto or molok meto.
- ISO 639-3 for Uab Meto: `aoz`. Glottocode: `uabm1237`. Baikeno is a separate ISO code, `bkx`, in the Wikipedia language table. Some catalogs also split Amarasi. Peta Bahasa still lists Amarasi as one dialect inside Dawan (Timor). Say which frame you are using.
- Peta Bahasa dialects: Kupang Timur, Amarasi, Fatule'u, Insana-Biboki-Pasebe, Timor Tengah Selatan, Amanatun, Miomafo Barat, Mallo Netpala, and Mallo Nenas.
- Peta Bahasa separates Dawan from Helong and Buna. Tetun is a different language on the same NTT map. Melayu Kupang is not this language.
- Source confidence: Level 2. Tata Bahasa Dawan (Tarno et al., 1992) is a real grammar. The reusable words below are from a later Amfoang comparison, not from a copied dictionary page.
- Speech habit: name the variety, then one word. Inside Amfoang, Oenames often keeps a closed syllable where Kaesmetan adds a vowel: hit and hita are both glossed 'we', nub and nua are both 'two'.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent vocabulary, ritual language, or titles.
- Do not treat Oenames, Kaesmetan, Amanuban, Mollo, or Amarasi as one speech.
- Do not write adat, wedding, or church text from a wordlist.
- Keep Indonesian when the Dawan sentence is not printed in a source.
- Ask for the village name.
- Do not mix Tetun or Melayu Kupang into a Dawan line.
- Do not use leko or naleok as "sama-sama". In the Amfoang list, leko is 'sehat' and naleok is 'handsome'.

## Sample Prompts

- "Use $tutur-dawan to review this Bahasa Dawan text."
- "Open a note for an Amfoang cousin and label Oenames or Kaesmetan."
- "Check whether leko was used as thanks or as sehat."
- "Give the two Amfoang forms for two, and do not pick one for Soe."
- "Keep the clinic time in Indonesian and add only Tabe."

## Safe Output Patterns

- Open with Tabe when a hello is needed, and say the phrase list does not name the variety.
- Use au, ho, leko, mese, and the hit/hita pair only with the Amfoang subdialect label.
- For review, flag Tetun, Melayu Kupang, and the thanks-reading of leko.
- For unknown terms, keep Indonesian and name the gap.
- For public use, ask a speaker from that village.
- For admin facts, keep time, place, and money in Indonesian.
- Mark mixed lines as mixed.
