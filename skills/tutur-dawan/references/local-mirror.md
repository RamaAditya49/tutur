# Local Mirror

## Source Mirror

- Language: Bahasa Dawan (Timor) on Peta Bahasa. Autonym in the Amfoang field study: uab meto or molok meto. Catalog names also include Atoni and Uab Meto.
- ISO 639-3: `aoz` for Uab Meto. Glottocode: `uabm1237`. Wikipedia's language table also lists Baikeno as `bkx`. Do not generate Baikeno, and do not use Oecussi Portuguese loans such as obrigadu, from a West Timor wordlist.
- Region: West Timor in NTT, plus the Ambenu area named on Peta Bahasa. This skill's worked words are not an Oecussi set.
- Nine Peta Bahasa dialects are listed in `offline-brief.md`. Village nicknames for the language differ inside one kabupaten.
- A 1992 grammar, Tata Bahasa Dawan, exists from Pusat Pembinaan dan Pengembangan Bahasa. A later journal says its own Dawan examples are Amanuban. Those papers are identity sources. Their sentences are not copied here because this skill did not lift a clean example sentence from them.
- Wordlist used for generation: Oenames and Kaesmetan, two subdialects of the Amfoang dialect, in the 2025 Humanis comparison. Both keep au, ho, in, hai, sin, leko, mese, naek, and muti. They differ on hit/hita and on several numbers.
- Phrase-list support: English Wikipedia Uab Meto table for Tabe, Hao, and Kaha'. That table also puts leko and naleok in a "you are welcome" row. The Amfoang field study glosses those words differently. The field study wins for those two words.
- Source confidence: Level 2 because the 1992 grammar is real and the 2025 paper prints glossed words. Coverage of a whole valley is still thin.

Source links are in `sources.md`. This file does not copy a dictionary.

## Local Usability

Use this file offline. A chat may start with Tabe and continue in Indonesian. That message is mixed. It is not Uab Meto throughout.

The practical speech habit is to say the variety before the word. Amfoang Oenames and Kaesmetan already disagree on 'we' and on 'two'. Amanuban, Mollo, Amarasi, and a Kupang Timur village are further away. Peta Bahasa's nine dialect names and Tarno's older dialect discussion are not the same list. Do not merge them.

Melayu Kupang is the town speech people may expect if the user only says "Kupang". Tetun is the language of the Malaka side in the Peta Bahasa comparison, not Dawan. Helong and Buna are the languages Peta Bahasa uses to show Dawan is separate.

## Vocabulary And Semantics

Verified items only.

Amfoang, both Oenames and Kaesmetan, Humanis 2025:

- au: 'I'.
- ho: 'you'.
- in: 'he/she'. No gender split in that table.
- hai: 'we'.
- sin: 'they'.
- leko: 'healthy'. Not a thanks reply.
- naleok: 'handsome' in the same adjective table. Not a thanks reply.
- naek: 'big'.
- muti: 'white'.
- men: 'sick'.
- mese: 'one'.
- ha: 'four'.
- ne: 'six'.
- boes: 'ten'.

Amfoang, different by subdialect, same paper:

- hit: 'we' in Oenames. hita: 'we' in Kaesmetan. The paper glosses both as we and does not say which is inclusive. Do not add that meaning.
- nub: 'two' in Oenames. nua: 'two' in Kaesmetan.
- teun: 'three' in Oenames. tenu: 'three' in Kaesmetan.
- nim: 'five' in Oenames. nima: 'five' in Kaesmetan.

Wikipedia phrase table, variety not stated. Support only:

- Tabe: listed with Halo as hello.
- Hao: yes, marked normal on that table. Pah and Tua are marked polite yes, He' informal yes, Ya normal yes. Do not pick one for a named village.
- Kaha': no. Kahfa' is the other form in the same cell.

Do not take numbers from that Wikipedia table (Mese', Nua, Teun) and mix them with the Amfoang pair above. The spellings do not match one variety.

## Generation Rules

1. Write the meaning in plain Indonesian first.
2. Add only Dawan pieces listed above, and label Amfoang Oenames, Amfoang Kaesmetan, or "phrase list, variety unstated".
3. If a full sentence is not attested, keep it in Indonesian and label the message mixed.
4. Do not read leko or naleok as terima kasih or sama-sama.
5. Do not export hit, hita, nub, or nua to Amanuban, Mollo, or Amarasi.
6. Do not use Tetun, Melayu Kupang, or Baikeno obrigadu.
7. For public or professional text, keep time, place, price, and policy in Indonesian.
8. If the user names Tetun or Melayu Kupang, stop and say this skill is Dawan / Uab Meto only.
