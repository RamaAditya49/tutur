# Style Guide

## Language Identity

- Name: Bahasa Dawan, Uab Meto, Atoni. Folder name: tutur-dawan.
- ISO 639-3 `aoz`. Glottocode `uabm1237`. Baikeno `bkx` is not this set of examples.
- Area: Timor, NTT. Peta Bahasa name: Bahasa Dawan (Timor).
- Nine dialects to ask about: Kupang Timur, Amarasi, Fatule'u, Insana-Biboki-Pasebe, Timor Tengah Selatan, Amanatun, Miomafo Barat, Mallo Netpala, Mallo Nenas.
- Worked wordlist: Amfoang subdialects Oenames and Kaesmetan only.
- Neighbors it is confused with: Bahasa Tetun, and Melayu Kupang in town. Peta Bahasa also separates Dawan from Helong and Buna.
- Speech habit to name: say the variety. In Amfoang, a closed syllable in Oenames often has a vowel added in Kaesmetan.

## Safety Rules

- Do not invent vocabulary, ritual language, or titles.
- Do not let one valley stand for Uab Meto.
- Do not write adat, legal, or official text from a wordlist.
- Keep Indonesian when the Dawan sentence is unknown.
- Ask for a sample when the user wants Amanuban, Mollo, Amarasi, or Baikeno.
- Do not mix those varieties, and do not mix in Tetun or Melayu Kupang.
- Do not use the Wikipedia welcome-row for leko or naleok.

## Practical Use

Use this skill for source-aware Dawan openings, Amfoang word checks, and review of machine text. It fits chat and short community notes. It does not fit a Dawan-only announcement.

## Registers

### Everyday

Phrase-list hello, variety unstated:

> Tabe.

Amfoang single words, label the subdialect:

- leko 'sehat' (Oenames and Kaesmetan).
- au 'saya', ho 'kamu' (both).
- hit 'we' only for Oenames. hita 'we' only for Kaesmetan.

The errand after Tabe is Indonesian. Mark the message mixed.

### Public

Use Tabe, then Indonesian for the time and place. Do not address a whole desa with ho unless the variety is Amfoang and the user asked for that pronoun as a citation, not as a new sentence.

### Professional

For school, clinic, or village office, keep the deadline in Indonesian. Hao and Kaha' are yes and no on the phrase list, variety unstated. They can answer a yes-no question as single words. They do not carry a policy sentence.

### Adat

Do not compose Atoni adat speech, marriage formulas, or church lines. An Amanuban grammar paper exists. Its existence is not a script. Ask for the text the family already uses, and name the village.
