# Offline Brief

## Local Facts

- Purpose: ordinary messages, short greetings, and review for Bahasa Tombulu.
- Region: Tomohon and Minahasa villages in the Tombulu area, Sulawesi Utara. Peta Bahasa records the Tombulu dialect at Lemoh (Tombariri Timur) and Woloan Dua (Tomohon Barat).
- Identity split: Peta Bahasa puts Tombulu inside Bahasa Minahasa, with Tountemboan and Toulour. ISO 639-3 `tom` and Glottolog `tomb1243` list Tombulu as its own language.
- Not Melayu Manado. Not Tonsea. Not Tontemboan / Tountemboan. Not Toulour. Not Tonsawang.
- Source confidence: Level 2. Struktur Bahasa Tombulu (1993) and Fonologi Bahasa Tombulu (1994) are real grammars. Everyday greetings also sit in a public phrase table.
- Speech habit named in sources: Tombulu l is described as unlike the l of the other Minahasan languages. One public note gives kulo 'putih' as the example.
- Spelling is not officially fixed. The 2004 reading guide says a writing standard had not been set. Keep the source spelling.
- Attested short forms to reuse: Syambae; Kura-mo? leos 'man?; Niko; Makapulu Leos; tou 'orang'; wale 'rumah'; kuman 'makan'.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent vocabulary, titles, or ritual lines.
- Do not treat one village as the speech of all Tombulu speakers.
- Do not write church liturgy, adat speech, or a full translation from a greeting plus Indonesian grammar.
- Keep Indonesian when the Tombulu word is missing.
- Ask for a user sample when the village or the audience matters.
- Do not mix Tombulu with Tonsea, Tontemboan, or Toulour in one line.
- Malesung script and prayer text are out of scope unless the user supplies the source text.

## Sample Prompts

- "Use $tutur-minahasa-tombulu to review this Bahasa Tombulu text."
- "Make a short Tombulu-aware chat for a cousin in Tomohon."
- "Check whether this is Tombulu or Melayu Manado with a few local words."
- "Open a neighbor reminder with the attested Tombulu greeting only."
- "Say which spelling of 'baik' the grammar uses, and do not normalize it."

## Safe Output Patterns

- Open with Syambae or Kura-mo? when a greeting is needed, then give the practical point in Indonesian.
- For review, flag Manado Malay, the wrong Minahasan language, and unattested full sentences.
- For unknown terms, keep Indonesian and name the gap.
- For public use, ask a Tombulu speaker or a language institution to read it.
- For admin, school, prices, and times, keep the facts in Indonesian.
- Mark mixed lines as mixed.
