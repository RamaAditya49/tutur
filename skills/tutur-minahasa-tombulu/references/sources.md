# Sources

Language: Tombulu

Region/context: Tomohon and Minahasa, Sulawesi Utara. Peta Bahasa maps the Tombulu dialect at Lemoh and Woloan Dua and groups it under Bahasa Minahasa with Tountemboan and Toulour.

## Source Confidence

Level 2: guardrail and register skill. Struktur Bahasa Tombulu (1993) and Fonologi Bahasa Tombulu (1994) are published grammars from Pusat Pembinaan dan Pengembangan Bahasa. ISO 639-3 and Glottolog fix the code. A public phrase table supplies short greetings. Raise the level only after a dialect-labeled dictionary and speaker-reviewed lines are added here.

## Editorial Position

Ordinary chat can use the short forms that the grammar or the phrase table actually prints. Public, church, adat, legal, and official wording needs a Tombulu speaker.

The phrase table is support. It is not a license to translate a paragraph or to copy the prayers on that page. Peta Bahasa and Glottolog disagree on whether Tombulu is a dialect or a language. Say so instead of picking one in silence.
