# Tutur Tombulu

## Overview

Use this skill for Bahasa Tombulu in Tomohon, Tombulu, Tombariri, and nearby Minahasa villages named in the sources. Short greetings and single words are usable when a grammar or phrase table attests them. A full Tombulu paragraph is not.

Peta Bahasa lists Tombulu as one dialect of Bahasa Minahasa, beside Tountemboan and Toulour, and treats Tonsea and Tonsawang as separate comparison languages. Glottolog and ISO 639-3 list Tombulu itself as `tom` / `tomb1243`. Say which frame you are using. Do not answer in Melayu Manado and call it Tombulu.

## Search And Discovery

Relevant searches: Bahasa Tombulu, kamus Bahasa Tombulu, contoh Bahasa Tombulu, belajar Bahasa Tombulu, terjemahan Indonesia Tombulu, translate Bahasa Tombulu, Tombulu Tomohon, Toumbulu, Tombalu, Minahasa, AI skill Bahasa Tombulu.

Use this skill for source-aware Tombulu generation and review. It is not a certified translator. Dialect, adat, church, public, legal, educational, or official text stays with a Tombulu speaker when the wording is not in the local sources.

## Human Voice

Syambae. Kura-mo? Kalau menyapa saudara di Tomohon, buka dengan sapaan pendek itu, lalu langsung ke urusan: jam, tempat, atau barang yang dititip. Di kota, orang Tombulu sering sudah campur Melayu Manado, jadi jangan menempel kata Tombulu pada kalimat Manado. Sumber menyebut bunyi l Tombulu tidak sama dengan Tonsea, Tontemboan, atau Toulour. Kalimat yang belum ada di tata bahasa atau tabel frasa, tulis dalam bahasa Indonesia.

## Workflow

1. Identify the task.
   - Translation needs a source sample or a dictionary or grammar hit.
   - Style requests keep the Indonesian meaning and add only attested Tombulu.
   - Ask which village if the user names Minahasa without saying Tombulu.

2. Check source confidence.
   - Read `references/sources.md` first.
   - Treat Struktur Bahasa Tombulu (1993) as the grammar anchor.
   - Treat the Wikipedia phrase table as support for short greetings, not as a full dictionary.
   - If spelling differs (`le?os`, `le'os`, `leos`), show the source form and do not invent a standard.

3. Compose with source discipline.
   - Keep names, places, titles, dates, and facts unchanged.
   - Use verified Tombulu words only.
   - If the sentence is not attested, write Indonesian and label the result mixed.

4. Final pass.
   - Say what is attested and what stays in Indonesian.
   - Do not claim native-speaker or official authority.
   - Do not swap in Tonsea, Tontemboan, Toulour, or Melayu Manado.

## Output Style

- For a direct request, give the message first.
- For a review, name the risk, then give a safer rewrite.
- For public, church, legal, school, or government text, ask for a Tombulu speaker review.
- Label mixed Tombulu-Indonesian text as mixed.
