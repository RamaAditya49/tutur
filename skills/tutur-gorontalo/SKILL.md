# Tutur Gorontalo

## Overview

Use this skill to produce, adapt, or review Bahasa Gorontalo for ordinary messages. Speakers also call it Hulontalo. Peta Bahasa places it in Bone Bolango, Gorontalo Utara, and Kota Gorontalo, with a Bolango dialect in Bolaang Mongondow, Sulawesi Utara. ISO 639-3 `gor`, Glottocode `goro1259`. It is a Gorontalic language, not Melayu Manado and not a Minahasa dialect. Peta Bahasa puts the difference from other languages in the province, including Minahasa, at about 81%–100%.

Peta Bahasa names four dialects: Suwawa, Atinggola, Kota, and Bolango. Glottolog's dialect set (Kota, Limboto, Tilamuta, East, West) is a different classification. Do not merge the two lists into one spelling.

Use this skill for source-aware Gorontalo generation and review. It is not a certified translator. Adat, ritual, public, legal, educational, and official text stay under competent speaker review. Do not satisfy a Gorontalo request with Melayu Manado particles.

## Human Voice

Orang Hulontalo menanyakan kabar dengan `Wolo habali?`, dan memakai `Woloolo habali olanto?` kalau lawan bicaranya dijaga. Terima kasih yang tercatat di buku percakapan adalah `Odu'olo`, sering nempel di ujung kalimat Indonesia. Pagi dibuka `Paladu u dumodupo`, bukan sapaan Melayu Manado. Orang yang sudah menikah, dalam catatan yang mengutip kamus Pateda, disapa `Temei` atau `Tilei` ditambah nama kemenakan. Dialek Kota, Suwawa, Atinggola, dan Bolango jangan dijadikan satu ejaan.

## Workflow

1. Identify the task.
   - Translation needs a line from the conversation book, the Pateda notes in this skill, or a user sample.
   - Style adaptation keeps the facts in Indonesian.
   - If the user writes in Melayu Manado (`sto`, `kong`, `dang`) and asks for Gorontalo, do not treat those particles as Hulontalo.

2. Check source confidence.
   - Read `references/sources.md` first.
   - Prefer the Baruadi conversation book for greetings and thanks, and Pateda 1977 for `wau` and `yio`.
   - Keep `Temei` / `Tilei` as address terms reported from Pateda's dictionary. Do not invent a new teknonym.

3. Compose with source discipline.
   - Keep names, places, dates, and numbers unchanged.
   - Open with an attested `Paladu` or `Wolo habali` line, then say the practical point in Indonesian if the rest is not attested.
   - The conversation book prints several spellings (`Wolo` / `Wololo` / `Woloolo`, `Odu'olo` / `odu'ola`). Do not invent a seventh spelling.
   - Label mixed Gorontalo-Indonesian text as mixed.

4. Final pass.
   - Check that the text is not Melayu Manado with one Gorontalo word attached.
   - Do not claim native-speaker or official authority.

## Output Style

- For a direct request, return the message first.
- For a review, flag Manado Malay interference and unattested Gorontalo.
- For public or admin text, keep schedules, money, and documents in Indonesian.
- Mark mixed lines as mixed.
