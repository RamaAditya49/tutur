# Sources

Language: Gorontalo (Hulontalo)

Region/context: Kabupaten Bone Bolango, Kabupaten Gorontalo Utara, Kota Gorontalo, plus dialek Bolango in Bolaang Mongondow, Sulawesi Utara

ISO 639-3: gor

Glottocode: goro1259

## Source Confidence

Level 2: guardrail/register skill. A real dictionary exists: Mansoer Pateda, Kamus Bahasa Gorontalo-Indonesia (Departemen Pendidikan dan Kebudayaan, 1977; Balai Pustaka, 2001, ISBN 979-666-638-3). A teaching conversation book by Mohamad Karmin Baruadi supplies the greetings and thanks used here. Address terms `Temei` and `Tilei` are taken from a news report of Pateda's dictionary, so they stay support-level until a speaker sample confirms the teknonym for a named person.

## Editorial Position

Greetings, thanks, and the pronouns `wau` and `yio` may be used as short phrases. Do not copy the Pateda dictionary into an answer. Do not write Melayu Manado and label it Hulontalo. Adat formulas and marriage-ritual speech need a speaker sample. The two dialect lists, Peta Bahasa and Glottolog, are both recorded and not merged.
