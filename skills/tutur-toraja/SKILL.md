# Tutur Toraja

## Overview

Use this skill for ordinary Bahasa Toraja. The catalog name is Toraja-Sa'dan, ISO 639-3 `sda`, glottocode `tora1261`. Glottolog lists Tae', Ta'e, and Sa'dan among its alternative names. Badan Bahasa calls the language Bahasa Toraja and counts eleven dialects. Sak (Sa') and Tae is one of those dialects, mapped to Larompong in Luwu and to Malangke in Luwu Utara. It is not the label for Makale, Sesean, Sangalla, or the other Tana Toraja and Toraja Utara dialects on the same map.

The grammar examples here come from Tata Bahasa Toraja (1997), a Pusat Bahasa project volume. Those examples are not tagged to one kecamatan in the pages used here. Do not relabel them as Tae' only, or as Kesu' only. The visit greeting `manasumo raka?` comes from a 2024 article on Toraja social use. It is ordinary doorstep talk, not a funeral text.

Source confidence: Level 2. The 1997 grammar is a real grammar, and the greeting is published with a gloss. The Tammu and van der Veen dictionary (1972) exists and was not opened, so none of its pages are copied here.

## Human Voice

Orang masuk ke rumah tidak membuka dengan selamat pagi. Sapaan yang tercatat adalah `manasumo raka?`, kira-kira "apakah nasi sudah matang?", dan balasannya yang tertulis `io manasumo` atau `manasumo ta lendu' opa`. Milik ditandai di ekor kata: `puntingku` pisang saya, `banuammu` rumahmu, `banuanna` rumahnya. Tanya memakai `-raka`, seperti `ikoraka`. Kalau sisa maksudnya belum ada di tata bahasa ini, lanjutkan dalam bahasa Indonesia. Jangan susun ucapan Rambu Solo atau aluk.

## Workflow

1. Identify the task.
   - If the user names Makale, Rantepao, Sesean, Sangalla, Sa'dan, or Tae'/Luwu, keep that name on the output.
   - If they do not name a place, call the grammar lines Bahasa Toraja / Toraja-Sa'dan and do not claim a village dialect.
   - Ceremony, marriage, and funeral requests stop at the boundary below.

2. Check source confidence.
   - Use `references/sources.md`.
   - Grammar spelling in the 1997 book writes the glottal with `q` (`boqboq`, `doiq`). The 2024 greeting article uses an apostrophe (`lendu'`, and `bo'bo'` inside a note on cooking rice). Do not silently convert one spelling into the other.

3. Compose with source discipline.
   - Keep names, dates, places, and sums as written.
   - A full Toraja sentence that is not in the sources stays unwritten.
   - Label mixed text as mixed.

4. Final pass.
   - State the variety limit in one line when the text may be read in public.
   - Do not add Rambu Solo, aluk, or other ritual formulas.

## Output Style

- Direct request: message first.
- Review request: unsupported words first, then a shorter rewrite.
- Public request: Indonesian carries the instruction.
