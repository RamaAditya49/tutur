# Style Guide

## Language Identity

- Catalog variety: Toraja-Sa'dan, ISO 639-3 `sda`, glottocode `tora1261`.
- Alternative names on Glottolog include Tae', Ta'e, and Sa'dan. Those names do not mean every dialect in Tana Toraja is Tae'.
- Badan Bahasa: Bahasa Toraja, eleven dialects. Sak (Sa') and Tae is the dialect mapped in Larompong, Pattimang, and Takkalala, in Luwu and Luwu Utara.
- Grammar lines in this skill are from Tata Bahasa Toraja (1997) and are not village-tagged here.
- Greeting lines are ordinary visit language from Welem 2024.

## Safety Rules

- Do not invent vocabulary or cultural formulas.
- Do not write Rambu Solo or aluk text. A short ordinary word that also appears in ceremony talk, such as `banua` "rumah", may be used only in its ordinary gloss, with ceremony marked review-only.
- Do not borrow Bugis `tabe'` or Makassar particles.
- Keep legal, medical, school, and government details in Indonesian.
- Ask for the kabupaten or kecamatan when the text will be printed.

## Registers

### Everyday

Doorstep: `manasumo raka?` Family possession stays inside printed forms such as `puntingku` and `banuammu`.

### Public

A community reminder may open with the greeting, then give the time and place in Indonesian. Do not translate the whole notice.

### Professional

Office and school messages stay in Indonesian. A Toraja opening is optional and must be one of the published greetings, labeled as visit language rather than as an official formula.

### Adat

Rambu Solo, aluk, and other ritual speech are review-only. If the user pastes a family text, flag words that this skill cannot confirm. Do not complete the ceremony from imagination.
