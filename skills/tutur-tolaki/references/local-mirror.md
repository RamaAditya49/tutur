# Local Mirror

## Source Mirror

- Language: Bahasa Tolaki. Older name in the Peta Bahasa note: Tolaki Laiwui.
- ISO 639-3: `lbw`. Glottocode: `tola1247`. Glottolog status comment: threatened, 6b.
- Family, from the ABVD classification attached to this code: Austronesian, Malayo-Polynesian, Celebic, Eastern, Southeastern, Bungku-Tolaki, Western, West Coast. Not Wotu-Wolio. Not Muna.
- Region: Kolaka, Kolaka Utara, Konawe, Konawe Utara, Konawe Selatan, Kolaka Timur, and some places in Kota Kendari.
- Dialects, Peta Bahasa: (1) Mekongga, (2) Rahambuu, (3) Kodeoha, (4) Konawe, (5) Laromerui, (6) Waru. Inter-dialect difference about 56.78%–75.54%.
- Contact, not identity: Bugis beside Tolaki in Kolaka and Kolaka Utara; Culambacu in Konawe Utara and Konawe; Sunda, Jawa, and Bali communities in Konawe Selatan.
- Alternative names recorded by Glottolog include Laki, Lalaki, Lolaki, To'olaki, and Kolaka. Do not treat those labels as extra dialects.
- Phrase sources: Dirjan and Ino 2019 (politeness) and Haslan 2018 (address terms). Dictionary of record: Sambari 2015, not copied here. Function-word study: Muthalib dkk. 1985, not copied here.

## Local Usability

This file is the offline substitute for source orientation. It does not copy a dictionary. It tells the agent which short Tolaki pieces are glossed, which dialect split matters, and which requests stay in Indonesian.

Ordinary chat and a neighborhood note may open with `tabea` or `tabee`. A full Tolaki announcement, a noble address, and any adat formula need a speaker sample. Kendari city speech is not a seventh dialect in Peta Bahasa. Some Tolaki is spoken there beside other languages.

## Vocabulary And Semantics

Only items with a published gloss:

- `tabea` / `tabee`: permisi. Dirjan and Ino use both spellings. `tabee` marks the polite request. Do not pick a third spelling.
- `tabea inggomiu`: elder-to-elder softener. Their example is `tabea inggomiu perikeikona pani Dewi`, glossed "tanpa mengurangi rasa hormat, bisa panggilkan bapaknya Dewi". They also say the piece involves speaker and hearer together ("kita"). Do not use it downward to a child.
- `Tabee, keno oki mobea`: opening of the polite request, glossed "Permisi, kalau tidak berat". The rest of their hoe sentence is about borrowing a tool. Do not reuse the tool clause for a new object.
- `salamunggu toka`: "Saya titip salam". The reply in the same data is `Ku onggoki moko dunggui salamumu`, "Saya akan sampaikan salammu".
- `ina`: ibu. `ama`: bapak. `naina`: bibi/tante. `ma'ama`: paman. Haslan 2018 table.
- `pue`, `mbue`: kakek, nenek, and also cucu in that table. The sample exchange uses `pue` from the grandchild's side. Do not reduce the pair to one English kin term.
- `ka'aka`: kakak. `ka'aka langgai` kakak laki-laki, `ka'aka ndina` kakak perempuan, in the same paper.
- `hai`: adik in the findings table. `hai langgai` adik laki-laki, `hai ndina` adik perempuan.
- `ana`: anak. Sample `Ino ananggu`, paper gloss "This my son" (ini anakku).
- Sample question `Tina, embee ariamu?`, paper gloss "Tina, where are you going?" Reply `Arilako mo'oli osampo`, "I'm going to buy a shampoo". Quote these. Do not swap in a new destination.

## Generation Rules

1. Write the meaning in plain Indonesian first.
2. Add only the Tolaki pieces listed above, or a user sample.
3. Open with `tabea` or `tabee` when the message asks permission or softens a request.
4. Keep place names such as Kendari, Konawe, and Mekongga as names. Do not translate them.
5. If the user names Mekongga, Rahambuu, Kodeoha, Laromerui, or Waru, say that the glossed samples are not labeled as that dialect and keep the body in Indonesian.
6. Mark mixed text as mixed.
7. For public copy, one Tolaki opener plus Indonesian facts.
8. For adat or noble speech, stop and ask for the local line.

## Starter Lexicon Policy

Add a Tolaki word only after a gloss is found in a dictionary example, a grammar, or a published utterance. Do not bulk-copy Sambari 2015. Do not mine the ABVD list to build sentences.

## Register Handling

- Everyday: `tabee` plus Indonesian, family terms `ina`, `ama`, `ka'aka`, `hai`.
- Between elders: `tabea inggomiu` is the attested softener, still not a full speech.
- Public: Indonesian carries time, place, and duty. Tolaki carries the opener.
- Professional: documents and deadlines stay in Indonesian.
- Adat: no composed ritual line. Noble kinship address reported for Wawotobi is not generated here.
