# Sources

Language: Tolaki

Region/context: Kabupaten Kolaka, Kolaka Utara, Konawe, Konawe Utara, Konawe Selatan, Kolaka Timur, dan sebagian Kota Kendari, Sulawesi Tenggara

ISO 639-3: lbw

Glottocode: tola1247

## Source Confidence

Level 2: guardrail/register skill. A real dictionary exists (Kamus bahasa Tolaki-Indonesia, Sambari, Pustaka Larasan bersama Dinas Pendidikan dan Kebudayaan Kabupaten Konawe, 2015) and a Pusat Bahasa function-word study exists (Kata Tugas dalam Bahasa Tolaki, 1985). This skill does not copy those books. Short phrases below come from two published studies that gloss them. Raise confidence for a full sentence only when a speaker sample or a dictionary example is in hand.

## Editorial Position

Ordinary messages may use the short glossed phrases in `examples.md`. Mekongga, Rahambuu, Kodeoha, Konawe, Laromerui, and Waru are not one spelling. Adat speech, noble kinship address, and any full paragraph stay with a Tolaki speaker.

Do not treat a travel list or a Muna/Wolio phrase as Tolaki. Burhanuddin 1979, as cited by Peta Bahasa, also used the name Tolaki Laiwui with two dialects, Tolaki and Laiwui. That older split is not a license to invent a third name.
