# Tutur Maanyan

## Overview

Use this skill for Bahasa Maanyan, the Barito language Peta Bahasa records in Kalimantan Tengah and Kalimantan Selatan. Survey points are Desa Batapah (Timpah, Kapuas), Desa Malungai (Gunung Timang, Barito Utara), and Desa Warukin (Tanta, Tabalong). Peta Bahasa calls Batapah and Malungai two dialects (perbedaan 70,05%) and treats Warukin as the same language (beda subdialek, 48%).

It is not Bahasa Dayak Ngaju. Peta Bahasa puts the difference with Ngaju at 95,75%, with Banjar at 95%, and with Lawangan at 92%. Balai Bahasa Kalimantan Selatan also notes that Warukin sits in a Banjar settlement pocket, so speakers switch. Switching is not a reason to fill gaps with Banjar words.

## Human Voice

Amahku tulak bagawi. Begitu kabar keluarga: ayah sudah berangkat kerja, lalu sisanya bahasa Indonesia. Orang ini dikenalkan "ulun yiti hengaw anniku", rumah yang sudah tua "lewu yeru haut matueh". Yiti dan yeru nempel di kata yang ditunjuk, bukan tempelan di akhir kalimat Indonesia. Ini Maanyan harian, bukan Ngaju, dan bukan bahasa pangunraun yang mantir pakai untuk taliwakas. Di Warukin orang gampang pindah ke Banjar; jangan ditambal lalu disebut Maanyan.

## Workflow

1. Identify the task.
   - Translation needs a printed Maanyan sample for the place named.
   - Style requests keep the Indonesian meaning and add only attested Maanyan lines.
   - Ask Batapah, Malungai, Warukin, Tamiang Layang, Barito Timur, or Barito Selatan if the user says Maanyan without a place.

2. Check source confidence.
   - Read `references/sources.md` first.
   - Everyday lines in this skill come from the public teaching page, each with its Indonesian gloss. They are not entries copied from the Balai Bahasa dictionary.
   - Pangunraun, taliwakas, hiyang wadian, and kalakar are named in Linarto's article. Do not compose them.

3. Compose with source discipline.
   - Keep names, places, titles, dates, and facts unchanged.
   - Use verified Maanyan lines only.
   - If the sentence is not attested, write Indonesian and label the result mixed.

4. Final pass.
   - Say the line is from the teaching page when that is the source.
   - Do not claim native-speaker or official authority.
   - Do not answer in Ngaju, Banjar, Bakumpai, Lawangan, or Dusun.

## Output Style

- For a direct request, give the message first.
- For a review, name the risk, then give a safer rewrite.
- For public, adat, legal, school, or government text, ask for a Maanyan speaker from the named place.
- Label mixed Maanyan-Indonesian text as mixed.
