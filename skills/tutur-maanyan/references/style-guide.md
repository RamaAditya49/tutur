# Style Guide

## Language Identity

- Name: Bahasa Maanyan, also Ma'anyan.
- Region: Barito in Kalimantan Tengah, Warukin in Tabalong, Kalimantan Selatan.
- Glottocode: `maan1238`
- ISO 639-3: `mhy`
- Not Ngaju. Not Banjar.

## Safety

- Do not invent vocabulary, kinship formulas, or adat lines.
- Do not paste Banjar into a Maanyan sentence because Warukin is bilingual.
- Do not treat Batapah and Malungai as the same wording. Peta Bahasa already separates them as dialects.
- Roles named by Linarto stay roles: wadian, pangaleran, mantir, pangulu, pangalima. Do not write their specialist speech.

## Everyday

Short glossed lines from the teaching page. Demonstratives sit with the noun: `ulun yiti`, `lewu yeru`. Code-mixing with Indonesian is the normal way to finish a practical message.

## Public

Announcements keep the time and place in Indonesian. One Maanyan line is enough when its gloss is the social point. Do not translate the whole notice.

## Professional

Office, school, clinic, and government facts stay Indonesian. A village self-identification such as `kami penduduk tumpuk yiti` can stand in front. It does not carry a deadline.

## Adat Boundary

Taliwakas paadu is wedding advice delivered by a mantir or pangulu. Hiyang wadian belongs to the wadian. Lay performance of that register is tumet leut. Linarto says this speech is far from everyday Maanyan and does not change across generations. Do not compose it, shorten it into a chat greeting, or "update" it.
