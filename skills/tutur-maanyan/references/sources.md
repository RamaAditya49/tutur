# Sources

Language: Maanyan (Ma'anyan)

Region/context: Kalimantan Tengah (Batapah in Kapuas; Malungai in Barito Utara; communities in Barito Timur and Barito Selatan) and Kalimantan Selatan (Warukin, Tabalong). Not Ngaju.

## Source Confidence

Level 2: a real dictionary exists, and a published article describes the ritual register. Kamus Dwibahasa Maanyan-Indonesia (Balai Bahasa Provinsi Kalimantan Selatan, entri 2022–2023, diumumkan 2025) and the 2018 Kamus dwibahasa Dayak Maanyan-Indonesia (Balai Bahasa Kalimantan Tengah) are the dictionary layer. The Drive file linked from the 2025 page was not publicly downloadable during this write-up, so no dictionary page is copied here and no dictionary example sentence is claimed. Everyday lines below come from a public teaching page that prints the Maanyan line and the Indonesian gloss together. Identity and dialect limits come from Peta Bahasa.

## Editorial Position

Ordinary chat may repeat a glossed line from the teaching page and must say so. The rest of a message stays Indonesian. Pangunraun, taliwakas paadu, hiyang wadian, and kalakar are not everyday Maanyan; Linarto says they are far from daily speech and are delivered by mantir, pangulu, or wadian. Do not extend those formulas.

Ngaju, Banjar, Bakumpai, and Lawangan stay outside this skill. Warukin diglossia with Banjar is a reason to keep Banjar words labeled Banjar.
