# Offline Brief

## Local Facts

- Purpose: short messages in Bahasa Maanyan, also spelled Ma'anyan.
- Codes: glottocode `maan1238`, ISO 639-3 `mhy`.
- Not Bahasa Dayak Ngaju. Peta Bahasa: difference with Ngaju 95,75%, Banjar 95%, Lawangan 92%, Bakumpai 94%.
- Peta Bahasa dialects: Batapah (Desa Batapah, Timpah, Kapuas) and Malungai (Desa Malungai, Gunung Timang, Barito Utara). Difference between those two: 70,05%.
- Warukin (Tanta, Tabalong, Kalimantan Selatan) is the same language, beda subdialek 48%.
- Balai Bahasa Kalimantan Selatan: Maanyan also in Barito Timur and Barito Selatan; Warukin is a pocket inside Banjar settlement, and speakers switch.
- Linarto, citing Kawi et al. 1980: Tamiang Layang is a center of Maanyan use. That does not erase Batapah, Malungai, or Warukin.
- Everyday lines in this skill are teaching-page items 1–40, each printed with an Indonesian gloss.
- Pangunraun is a separate high register. Lay recitation is tumet leut, wadian speech is hiyang wadian, mantir and pangulu speech is kalakar or taliwakas.
- This skill should work without browsing.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent vocabulary, titles, or taliwakas formulas.
- Do not treat Ngaju, Banjar, Bakumpai, or Lawangan words as Maanyan.
- Do not smooth Batapah, Malungai, and Warukin into one village style.
- Do not build a new sentence by swapping yiti and yeru into Indonesian syntax.
- One attested line, then Indonesian. Label mixed text as mixed.
- Wedding advice, wadian, and pangaleran speech stay with the person who holds that role.

## Sample Prompts

- "Use $tutur-maanyan to review this Bahasa Maanyan text."
- "Make a source-aware Bahasa Maanyan note to a cousin, then Indonesian for the time."
- "Check whether this is Maanyan or Ngaju with a few local words."

## Safe Output Patterns

- Name the place: Batapah, Malungai, Warukin, Tamiang Layang, or say the place was not given.
- Open with one glossed line whose meaning matches the message.
- Put hours, places, money, and forms in Indonesian.
- If the user wants taliwakas or a wadian text, stop and say that register is not generated here.
