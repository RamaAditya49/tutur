# Sources

Language: Nias (Li Niha)

Region/context: Pulau Nias and the mapped dialect villages in Nias, Nias Selatan, and Kota Gunungsitoli, Sumatera Utara

ISO 639-3: nia

Glottocode: nias1242

## Source Confidence

Level 2: a real dictionary exists. Pusat Pembinaan dan Pengembangan Bahasa published Kamus Nias-Indonesia in 1985 (Laiya, Zagoto, and others), and a later Kamus Pelajar Bergambar Nias-Indonesia is catalogued by the same repository. Worked phrases in this skill come from the Kemendikbud reader Ya'ahowu and from one glossed sentence in a Pengadilan Tinggi Agama Medan news note. The 1985 dictionary file was not opened for lemma quotes.

## Editorial Position

Open with Ya'ahowu and expect the same word back, as the Kemendikbud reader describes. Do not treat Gunungsitoli, Lahewa, Hilimboe, and Teluk Dalam as one spelling. Do not invent hoho, wedding, or healing formulas. Do not quote the 1985 dictionary from memory. Community pages that list saohagölö are support only. The court note is the glossed public sentence used here.
