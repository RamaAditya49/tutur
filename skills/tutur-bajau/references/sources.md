# Sources

Language: Bajau / Bajo

Region/context: Bahasa Bajo as named by Peta Bahasa (many shores in eastern Indonesia). Phrase spelling in this skill: Flores Sea Indonesian Bajo in Nuraini 2010, including Sapeken (Kangean, Sumenep), which is one point on that Peta Bahasa map. Not Bajau Pondong, Bajau Semayap, Bajau Tungkal Satu, Bajo Delang, or Sabah West Coast Bajau.

## Source Confidence

Level 2: a real grammar exists, Struktur Bahasa Bajo (Ni Luh Komang Candrawati and colleagues, Pusat Pembinaan dan Pengembangan Bahasa, 1997, ISBN 979 459 776 7). That book was not opened for example sentences here, so none are quoted from it. Usable words come from Chandra Nuraini, "Lexicon and word formation in Indonesian Bajo", Wacana 12(2), 2010, a published glossed description of the Flores Sea variety. The JIPA sketch of East Lombok (Tanjung Luar) is the same ISO code with another orthography. Identity limits come from Peta Bahasa and Glottolog.

## Editorial Position

Ordinary chat may drop in one Nuraini gloss and must name Flores Sea / Sapeken spelling. The rest stays Indonesian. East Lombok q-spelling is not mixed into those words. Pondong, Semayap, Tungkal Satu, Delang, and Sabah are outside the default. No ceremonial formula was taken from these sources, so none is written.
