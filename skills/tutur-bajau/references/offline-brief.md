# Offline Brief

## Local Facts

- Purpose: short messages in Bahasa Bajo, the Peta Bahasa name, Flores Sea words only.
- Codes for that Indonesian Bajau: glottocode `indo1317`, ISO 639-3 `bdl`.
- Not West Coast Bajau: `west2560`, ISO `bdr` (Sabah).
- Peta Bahasa also separates Bajau Pondong, Bajau Semayap, Bajau Tungkal Satu, and Bajo Delang.
- Inside Bahasa Bajo, the map is many villages. Named on the table used here: Dialek Kaung Bajo Tano (Labuan Lalar) and Samma (Jaya Bakti, Banggai). The NTT blurb is Nebe, Talibura, Sikka.
- Nuraini: self-name Sama, outsider name Bajo, language name Baun Same.
- Her word list is the Flores Sea variety: Kangean/Sapeken, Sulawesi Tenggara, Sumbawa, Flores. Sapeken is on the Peta Bahasa Bajo table.
- She treats Indonesian Bajo as mutually intelligible across that sea, with maybe two or three dialects. That claim does not erase the separate Peta Bahasa languages above.
- JIPA East Lombok (Tanjung Luar) is the same ISO code. Spelling uses q for the glottal stop. Do not mix it with Nuraini's apostrophe.
- No full Bajo sentence is held in this skill. Struktur Bahasa Bajo (1997) was not opened for quotes.
- This skill should work without browsing.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent vocabulary, boat blessings, or a pan-Bajau rite.
- Do not write Bajau Pondong, Semayap, or Tungkal Satu from these Flores Sea words.
- Do not copy Sabah Bajau sentences into an Indonesian Bajo message.
- Do not add ng- or ma- to a root to make a new verb. Nuraini describes that pattern; the examples here stay the glossed words she prints.
- One attested word, then Indonesian. Label mixed text as mixed.
- Do not treat Kaung Bajo Tano as the Sapeken list unless the user brings a Labuan Lalar sample.

## Sample Prompts

- "Use $tutur-bajau to review this Bahasa Bajo text."
- "Make a source-aware Bajo note that calls someone over, then Indonesian for the place."
- "Check whether this is Bahasa Bajo or Bajau Pondong."

## Safe Output Patterns

- Open with `ma'i` when the point is "come here".
- Use `nggai` when the point is "no".
- Put the hour and the pier in Indonesian.
- Say Flores Sea / Sapeken spelling, Bahasa Bajo on Peta Bahasa, not one village dialect.
