# Tutur Bajau

## Overview

Use this skill for Bahasa Bajo, the variety Peta Bahasa lists under that name across Bali, Jawa Timur, Sulawesi, Nusa Tenggara, and Maluku Utara (kode bahasa 060). The same map names at least Dialek Kaung Bajo Tano at Labuan Lalar and a Samma row at Jaya Bakti, Banggai. The NTT blurb is Desa Nebe, Talibura, Sikka. This is one public label over many shores, not one village dialect.

Peta Bahasa keeps other names as separate languages: Bajau Pondong (Berau, Paser, Penajam Paser Utara), Bajau Semayap (Kotabaru), Bajau Tungkal Satu (Tanjung Jabung Barat), and Bajo Delang (Flores Timur). Glottolog's Indonesian Bajau is glottocode `indo1317`, ISO 639-3 `bdl`. West Coast Bajau, Sabah, is `west2560` / `bdr` and is not this skill.

Speakers call themselves Sama. To outsiders they say Bajo. Nuraini records the language name Baun Same. Words in this skill are her Flores Sea list (Kangean/Sapeken, Sulawesi Tenggara, Sumbawa, Flores), which meets the Peta Bahasa Bajo point at Sapeken. East Lombok in the JIPA sketch is the same ISO code with a different spelling. Do not mix the two spellings in one line.

## Human Voice

Ma'i. Itu "mari" untuk Bajo Laut Flores yang Nuraini catat, lalu jam dan tempatnya bahasa Indonesia. Kalau tidak jadi, cukup nggai, terus jelaskan dalam bahasa Indonesia. Kampung disebut kampoh. Ke orang luar namanya Bajo; sesama mereka Sama, dan bahasanya Baun Same. Ini entri Bahasa Bajo di Peta Bahasa, ejaan Sapeken dan Laut Flores, bukan Bajau Pondong, bukan Bajau Semayap, bukan Bajau Tungkal Satu, dan bukan Bajau Sabah. Tidak ada gaya upacara satu untuk semua Bajau.

## Workflow

1. Identify the task.
   - Translation needs a printed sample of the named Bajo or Bajau variety.
   - Style requests keep the Indonesian meaning and add only attested words.
   - If the user says Bajau without a place, say this skill defaults to Peta Bahasa's Bahasa Bajo, Flores Sea spelling from Nuraini, anchored at Sapeken. Pondong, Semayap, Tungkal Satu, Delang, and Sabah are other requests.

2. Check source confidence.
   - Read `references/sources.md` first.
   - Nuraini 2010 is the word list. JIPA East Lombok is a second spelling of Indonesian Bajau, not a sentence bank to merge.
   - Struktur Bahasa Bajo (Pusat Bahasa, 1997) is a real grammar. Its example sentences were not opened for this skill, so they are not quoted.

3. Compose with source discipline.
   - Keep names, places, titles, dates, and facts unchanged.
   - Use verified Bajo words only. Do not add Bajo affixes to make a new sentence.
   - If the sentence is not attested, write Indonesian and label the result mixed.

4. Final pass.
   - Name Flores Sea / Sapeken when a Nuraini word is used.
   - Do not claim native-speaker or official authority.
   - Do not answer in Sabah Bajau, Bajau Pondong, or Bugis.

## Output Style

- For a direct request, give the message first.
- For a review, name the risk, then give a safer rewrite.
- For public, adat, legal, school, or government text, ask for a speaker from the named shore.
- Label mixed Bajo-Indonesian text as mixed.
