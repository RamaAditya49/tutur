# Sources

Language: Pakpak

Region/context: Sumatera Utara and Aceh. Peta Bahasa lists dialek Pakpak (Dairi) in Kabupaten Dairi and Kabupaten Tapanuli Utara. The USU Press book also maps Pakpak Bharat, Humbang Hasundutan, Aceh Singkil, and Subulussalam through five suak. Glottolog name: Batak Dairi. Not Karo. Not Batak Toba.

## Source Confidence

Level 2. Tindi Radja Manik, Kamus bahasa Dairi Pakpak–Indonesia, Pusat Pembinaan dan Pengembangan Bahasa, 1977, is a real dictionary. Its PDF was not opened line by line, so this skill does not quote headwords from it. Sentences in the examples come from a 2023 USU Press book that was read.

## Editorial Position

Ordinary chat may open with `njuah-njuah` and then stay in Indonesian. Do not import Horas, Mauliate, or Karo `mejuah-juah`. The book describes menutung tulan as a rite and names beings spoken to there. This skill does not write that utterance. Day-names in the old month list are likewise left in the book, not reused as a calendar. Community phrase lists that add kinship titles are not copied in.
