# Local Mirror

## Source Mirror

- Language: Bahasa Pakpak. Also called Dairi in the 1977 dictionary title and Batak Dairi on Glottolog.
- ISO 639-3: `btd`. Glottocode: `bata1294`.
- Peta Bahasa: dialek Pakpak (Dairi) inside Bahasa Batak. Named there: Kabupaten Dairi and Kabupaten Tapanuli Utara. Difference from dialek Toba: 75,25 percent.
- USU Press book read for sentences: Melisa Padang, Jekmen Sinulingga, Flansius Tampubolon, Aksara Pakpak, 2023. https://usupress.usu.ac.id/images/buku/Aksara%20Pakpak_layout_crop.pdf
- Dictionary of record, not line-checked here: Tindi Radja Manik, Kamus bahasa Dairi Pakpak–Indonesia, 1977. https://repositori.kemendikdasmen.go.id/27305/
- Source confidence: Level 2.
- The preface of the USU Press book closes with `Njuah Njuah`. The phonology section writes `njuah-njuah` and glosses it sehat-sehat, from `juah` kabar baik/sehat.

Source mirror notes:

- Pakpak is not Karo. Karo `mejuah-juah` is a different shape.
- Pakpak is not Toba. Horas and mauliate are not in the sentences below.
- Five suak are not one spelling. The book cites Berutu 2006 for Pegagan, Keppas, Boang, Kelasen, and Simsim as dialects.
- Two e vowels change meaning. Do not flatten them.

## Local Usability

Use this mirror for a chat, a short warning, or a review. One sentence from the book, then Indonesian.

`ko` is the you inside the warning sentences (`ulang ko`, `ndabuh ko`). Do not replace it with Karo `kam` or Simalungun `ham`.

`kita` in `kita karinana` is the group's we in the gloss kita semuanya. Do not build a new inclusive/exclusive rule from that one line.

The book also prints `leppa` lengket, `bettoh` tahu, `mbau` bau, `ndabuh` jatuh, `ngkapal` tebal, `mpostep` putus, `ntelpus` bocor, `ncor` pedas. Those are word glosses in the nasalisasi section. Do not join them into a sentence.

## Vocabulary And Semantics

Short notes from the 2023 book. The Indonesian line is the book's gloss.

- `njuah-njuah`: sehat-sehat. Nasal form of `juah`, kabar baik/sehat.
- `njuah-njuah mo kita karinana`: sehat-sehat lah kita semuanya. The book adds `i mo tuhu` and glosses that Amen. Do not add the Amen unless the user is actually in that closing.
- `njolmit ngo kutangku mi bekkasmu, kaduan pejumpa kita`: rumahku dekat ke tempatmu, suatu saat kita akan bertemu.
- `ulang ko menangkih-nagkih i benna terutung, i ndabuh ko nahan`: jangan kau panjati pokok durian itu, nanti kamu jatuh.
- `enggo nceda jembaten, oda boi ke karap i`: sudah rusak jembatan, tidak bisa lagi dilewati.
- `oda`: tidak, inside `oda boi`.
- `enggo`: sudah, inside `enggo nceda` and `enggo mpustak`.
- `mela`: malu. `mella`: kalau / jika.
- `mate`: mati. `keke`: bangkit. `mbereng`: hitam. Hard e in the book's reading note.
- `merampek`: renyah. `mbergoh`: dingin. Weak e.
- `tulanmu` in `mpihir pe tulanmu tapi mpihiren den ngo batu en`: tulangmu, glossed walaupun tulangmu keras, tapi tidak lebih keras dari batu. Not a kinship title.
- `suak`: the book says it is the marker of a Pakpak group, and that it can also mean dialek.

## Generation Rules

- Open with `njuah-njuah` or `njuah-njuah mo kita karinana`.
- Do not write Horas, Mauliate, Amang, Tulang, or `mejuah-juah`.
- Do not strip nasals.
- Do not turn the word-gloss list into a new sentence.
- Do not copy the menutung tulan description into a speech.
- Label mixed text as mixed.
- If the town is Sidikalang, say suak Keppas is the book's label for that area, and still do not claim the example sentence was recorded there.
