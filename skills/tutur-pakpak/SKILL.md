# Tutur Pakpak

## Overview

Use this skill for ordinary Bahasa Pakpak. Glottolog lists Batak Dairi as ISO 639-3 `btd`, glottocode `bata1294`. The 1977 Pusat Bahasa book is titled Kamus bahasa Dairi Pakpak–Indonesia. Peta Bahasa still files Pakpak (Dairi) as a dialect of Bahasa Batak, at 75,25 percent difference from dialek Toba, and names Kabupaten Dairi and Kabupaten Tapanuli Utara. A 2023 USU Press book on the script uses Bahasa Pakpak and maps five suak across Sumatera Utara and Aceh. This skill follows that Pakpak wording. It is not Karo and not Toba.

Source confidence: Level 2. The Manik dictionary exists and was not opened line by line. The sentences below were read from Padang, Sinulingga, and Tampubolon, Aksara Pakpak, USU Press, 2023.

## Human Voice

Salam yang tertulis di buku Aksara Pakpak adalah `njuah-njuah`, sehat-sehat, dan kalimatnya `njuah-njuah mo kita karinana`. Itu bukan `mejuah-juah` dan bukan horas. Kamu pada contoh larangan adalah `ko`, dalam `ulang ko menangkih-nagkih i benna terutung`. Suak perlu disebut karena Sim-sim, Keppas, Pegagan, Kelasen, dan Boang tidak satu ejaan. E pepet dan e taling mengubah arti: `mela` malu, `mella` kalau. Pada contoh fonologi, `tulanmu` artinya tulangmu, bukan sapaan paman.

## Workflow

1. Identify the task.
   - Ask which suak if the text will be read aloud: Sim-sim, Keppas, Pegagan, Kelasen, or Boang. The USU Press book maps them to Pakpak Bharat, Dairi, Humbang Hasundutan, and Aceh Singkil / Subulussalam.
   - If the user wants Karo or Toba, stop. Do not "fix" `njuah-njuah` into `mejuah-juah`.

2. Check source confidence.
   - Read `references/sources.md` before adding a Pakpak word.
   - Quote a sentence from the 2023 book. Do not mine the 1977 dictionary from memory.
   - Leave unknown words in Indonesian.

3. Compose with source discipline.
   - Keep names, places, dates, prices, and instructions unchanged.
   - Label mixed text as mixed.
   - Nasal forms in the book are part of the word: `njuah`, `ndabuh`, `nceda`. Do not strip the nasal to look simpler.

4. Final pass.
   - Name the source as the USU Press book when the line is one of its examples.
   - Do not claim the Manik dictionary was checked word by word.
   - Do not add a ritual formula from the menutung tulan description.

## Output Style

- For a direct request, give the message first.
- For a review, point at Karo or Toba wording, then give a shorter rewrite.
- For a public notice, keep the date, place, and instruction in Indonesian.
