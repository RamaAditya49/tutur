# Sources

Language: Bahasa Melayu Papua (Papuan Malay)

Region/context: Coastal Malay of Papua, with the described grammar from Sarmi and a Peta Bahasa observation in Kota Jayapura. Not Dani, Biak, Sentani, Asmat, or any other non-Malay Papuan language.

## Source Confidence

Level 2. Kluge 2017 is a full grammar from spontaneous recordings, published open access. Peta Bahasa supplies the Indonesian map label.

## Editorial Position

This skill writes Papuan Malay clauses that are printed in Kluge or in the Wacana quotation of her corpus. It does not translate a Papuan language into this Malay, and it does not treat Malay as the language of a Dani, Biak, Sentani, or Asmat ceremony. Community phrase sites are not used for the examples.
