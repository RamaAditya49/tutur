# Tutur Papua Malay

## Overview

Use this skill for Bahasa Melayu Papua only. It is a regional Malay and a language of wider communication along the coast, not a cover term for Papuan languages. Dani, Biak, Sentani, Asmat, and every other non-Malay language of Papua stay outside this skill. Peta Bahasa records Malay at Kampung Waena, Abepura, and Sentani in Kota Jayapura, and the longer map note calls that variety Melayu Papua. The place name Sentani on that map is not the Sentani language.

The working grammar is Angela Kluge, *A grammar of Papuan Malay*, based on recordings in the Sarmi area on the northeast coast. Glottolog: glottocode `papu1250`. ISO 639-3 `pmy`. Kluge states that this Malay is not the language of formal government, school, or religious preaching, while it is ordinary speech everywhere else, including unofficial talk inside formal settings.

Source confidence: Level 2 for clauses that appear in that grammar. New sentences stay mixed.

Use this skill for source-aware Melayu Papua. It is not a certified translator and it does not write Dani, Biak, Sentani, Asmat, Melayu Ambon, or Melayu Ternate.

## Human Voice

People pick out the listener by putting `ko` on a name or a kin term, as in `mama-ade ko` and `Wili ko`. I is `sa`. A man in the corpus is `pace`, a woman is `mace`, and setting off together is `sa dengang pace ini kitong dua jalang, ayo`. The cooking offer is specific: one person takes the meat, the speaker makes papeda. A pushy family line is `Nofela bicara suda`, with `suda` doing the pushing, and the child's warning uses `jangang`, not a borrowed Ambon `jang`. None of these lines is a slogan, and none of them is Dani, Biak, Sentani, or Asmat.

## Workflow

1. Identify the language.
   - Melayu Papua or Papuan Malay: use this skill.
   - Dani, Biak, Sentani, Asmat, or any other Papuan language: stop and say this skill is only Papuan Malay.
   - Melayu Ambon or Melayu Ternate: use the other skill.

2. Check source confidence.
   - Use `references/sources.md` first.
   - Prefer Kluge's corpus examples over community phrase lists.
   - Sarmi is the described variety. Do not claim the same wording for every Papua town.

3. Compose with source discipline.
   - Keep names, places, dates, and facts unchanged.
   - A full Melayu Papua sentence must be one Kluge recorded or a user sample.
   - Otherwise one attested clause, then Indonesian. Label it mixed.

4. Final pass.
   - Do not swap in `beta`, `ose`, or `dangke`. Those belong to Melayu Ambon.
   - Do not claim the text is fit for a government letter or a sermon.

## Output Style

- For direct requests, return the adapted output first.
- For review requests, list risks and then provide a safer rewrite.
- For public or official facts, keep the facts in Indonesian.
- Mark mixed text as mixed.
