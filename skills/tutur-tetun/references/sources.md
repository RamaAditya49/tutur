# Sources

Language: Bahasa Tetun as used in Belu, Malaka, and Timor, Indonesia

Region/context: Kabupaten Belu and Kabupaten Malaka, Nusa Tenggara Timur. Fehan (plain, around Betun and Besikama, including areas now in Malaka) and Foho (hill, including parts of Atambua and northern Belu) are the local variety labels. Tetun Dili is a separate register.

## Source Confidence

Level 2. A published Fehan grammar sketch and published household transcripts from Malaka Barat support short, sourced phrases. Glottolog gives one Tetum code. The Badan Bahasa Tetun textbook documents official Timor-Leste Tetun and is cited so it is not mistaken for Belu usage.

## Editorial Position

Quote Fehan and Foho lines for Indonesian requests. Quote the 2019 textbook only when the user asks for Tetun Dili or official Timor-Leste Tetum, and label that output Tetun Dili. Do not merge the two into one house style. Dawan and Kupang Malay are other languages.
