# Offline Brief

## Local Facts

- Purpose: short, source-aware messages in Bahasa Tetun of Belu and Malaka.
- Region: Kabupaten Belu and Kabupaten Malaka, Pulau Timor, Nusa Tenggara Timur.
- Peta Bahasa places Tetun in Lakekun, Kobalima, Malaka, with Dawan to the north and a dialectometry gap above 81 percent against Bunak and Dawan.
- Local labels in the sources: Fehan or Tetun Terik on the southern plain, Foho in the hills and parts of Atambua.
- Identity code in Glottolog: glottocode `tetu1245`, ISO 639-3 `tet`. That single code still covers more than one variety.
- Tetun Dili, also called Tetun Prasa, is the Timor-Leste lingua franca and official-language variety. It is not the default here.
- Household samples come from Uma Lor, Malaka Barat (Adnyana 2018) and from the Fehan sketch centred on Betun and Besikama.
- Attested answers: `He'e` (yes, already; women and men) and `Hi'i` (yes; women, informal only).
- Attested leaving line: `Mai Bapa ita lao` (ayo Bapak, kita berangkat).
- This skill should work without browsing.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent vocabulary, titles, or adat formulas.
- Do not answer a Belu or Malaka request with Tetun Dili textbook lines such as `Diak ka lae?`.
- Do not treat Dawan or Kupang Malay as Tetun.
- Do not mix Fehan spelling (`ha'u`, `la'o`) and Uma Lor spelling (`hau`, `lao`) inside one sentence.
- `Hi'i` is not a general yes. The Uma Lor study limits it to informal speech by women.
- Mother's calls `bete`, `bu'i`, `mane`, and `bo'o` are family address, not public titles.
- If a full sentence is not attested, write the short attested phrase and continue in Indonesian. Label it mixed.
- Adat speech, including makoan turns, stays with a local speaker.

## Sample Prompts

- "Use $tutur-tetun to review this Bahasa Tetun text for a family in Malaka."
- "Make a source-aware Bahasa Tetun version of this short message for Belu."
- "Check whether this text is Tetun Fehan or Tetun Dili."
- "Keep Dawan and Kupang Malay out of this Tetun reply."
- "Show a household yes, then put the meeting time in Indonesian."

## Safe Output Patterns

- Open with an attested household word, then the practical point.
- For a where-question about a named child, the attested shape is `Yani nabe?`.
- For an invitation to leave together, the attested shape is `Mai Bapa ita lao`.
- Put schedules, prices, places, and policy in Indonesian.
- Name the variety when two forms are both called Tetun.
- For public or adat use, ask for a speaker from that community.
