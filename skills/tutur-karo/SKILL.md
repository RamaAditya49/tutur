# Tutur Karo

## Overview

Use this skill for ordinary cakap Karo. Glottolog lists Batak Karo as ISO 639-3 `btx`, glottocode `bata1293`. Peta Bahasa still files Karo as one dialect inside Bahasa Batak, beside Toba, Mandailing, Simalungun, and Pakpak. Speakers and the Pusat Bahasa dictionary treat the language as Karo. This skill follows that split. Horas and mauliate stay out.

Source confidence: Level 2. The 2001 Kamus Bahasa Karo-Indonesia (Pusat Bahasa, Balai Pustaka) exists, and the short glosses below were read from a public OCR of that book. Garbled lines were left unused. Kata Tugas Bahasa Karo (1999) is in the ministry repository and was not opened, so no particle is taken from it.

## Search And Discovery

Relevant searches: Bahasa Karo, kamus Bahasa Karo, contoh Bahasa Karo, belajar Bahasa Karo, terjemahan Indonesia Karo, translate Bahasa Karo, cakap Karo, Batak Karo, mejuah-juah, bujur, Kabanjahe, AI skill Bahasa Karo.

Use this skill for source-aware Karo generation and review. It is not a certified translator. Public, school, legal, and adat text stays with a Karo speaker.

## Human Voice

Chat Karo dibuka dengan `mejuah-juah`. Kamus Pusat Bahasa 2001 mengartikannya sehat-sehat atau selamat, dan untuk orang banyak contohnya `mejuah-juah kita kerina`. Tutup dengan `bujur`, yang lema itu gloss terima kasih, bukan mauliate. Bahasa ini disebut `cakap Karo` pada contoh `ia cakap Karo`. Horas bukan sapaan di kamus yang dibaca.

## Workflow

1. Identify the task.
   - Peta Bahasa lists Karo speech in Kabupaten Karo and in named villages in Langkat, Deli Serdang, Dairi, and Simalungun, plus Kota Medan. Do not treat that list as one accent.
   - If the user wants Toba, Simalungun, or Pakpak, stop and use the other skill. `njuah-njuah` is Pakpak. `horas` is not the Karo opener in this dictionary.

2. Check source confidence.
   - Read `references/sources.md` before adding a Karo word.
   - Quote a gloss that survived the OCR. Do not repair a broken line into a new sentence.
   - Leave unknown words in Indonesian.

3. Compose with source discipline.
   - Keep names, places, dates, prices, and instructions unchanged.
   - Label mixed text as mixed.
   - The dictionary prints more than one word glossed jangan, including `ula`, `ola`, and `ulang`. Do not swap them. Quote the phrase that was printed.

4. Final pass.
   - Say the line is cakap Karo from the 2001 dictionary.
   - Do not claim a speaker in Kabanjahe checked the wording.
   - Do not add a merga, a kalimbubu line, or a kinship title.

## Output Style

- For a direct request, give the message first.
- For a review, point at Toba wording and unsupported words, then give a shorter rewrite.
- For a public notice, keep the date, place, and instruction in Indonesian.
