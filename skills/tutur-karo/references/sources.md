# Sources

Language: Karo

Region/context: Sumatera Utara. Peta Bahasa lists dialek Karo inside its Bahasa Batak entry, with villages in Kabupaten Karo, Langkat, Deli Serdang, Dairi, Simalungun, and Kota Medan. Glottolog lists a separate language. Not Batak Toba, not Simalungun, not Pakpak.

## Source Confidence

Level 2. Kamus Bahasa Karo-Indonesia, Ahmad Samin Siregar and others, Pusat Bahasa / Balai Pustaka, 2001, is a real dictionary. The glosses in this skill were read from a public OCR of that book. Lines that collided with the next entry were not quoted. Kata Tugas Bahasa Karo (Bangun, Isa, Bangun, and Widayati, 1999) is catalogued and was not opened.

## Editorial Position

Ordinary chat may open with `mejuah-juah` and close with `bujur`, then stay in Indonesian. Do not import Horas, Mauliate, Amang, or Tulang from Batak Toba. Merga address and ritual speech, including muncang kuta formulas, are out of scope. A news recap or a blog phrase list is not added on top of the dictionary.
