# Sources

Language: Mentawai

Region/context: Kabupaten Kepulauan Mentawai, Sumatera Barat. Peta Bahasa sample points: Monganpoula, Maileppet, Sioban, and Makalo.

ISO 639-3: mwv

Glottocode: ment1249

## Source Confidence

Level 2: real dictionary and grammar books exist. Pusat Bahasa published Bahasa Mentawai in 1978 (dialek Sikakap) and Kamus Bahasa Indonesia–Mentawai in 1998 (Khatib, editor Purba). Balai Bahasa Provinsi Sumatera Barat collected Mentawai vocabulary in Pagai Utara and Pagai Selatan in February 2021 for a dictionary. The glosses used in examples come from the Mentawai–Indonesia–English dictionary compiled by Panulis Saguntung, a native speaker who also sat on the 2025 Pemkab Mentawai dictionary panel. Office greeting names come from the district communications office.

## Editorial Position

Use abekia for the dictionary gloss "bagaimana, apa kabar". Use surak or sura' for terima kasih, and say the final k versus hamzah is the orthography problem the Saguntung preface already describes. Anaileuita and Maronsita are greeting names from the district news note. Do not assign them a tighter gloss than that note gives. Do not invent sikerei, punen, healing, or wedding formulas. Do not treat Siberut, Sipora, and Pagai as one spelling.
