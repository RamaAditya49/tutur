# Style Guide

## Language Identity

- Name: Bahasa Mentawai.
- Folder: tutur-mentawai.
- ISO 639-3 `mwv`. Glottocode `ment1249`.
- Area: Kepulauan Mentawai. Sample points in Peta Bahasa sit on Siberut, Sipora, and Pagai.
- Older grammar sample: dialek Sikakap (1978). Do not treat that sample as the speech of every island.
- Not this skill: Minangkabau, and not a single Mentawai spelling for Siberut, Sipora, and Pagai.
- Speech habit to name: island difference first. A Tuapejat counter may show Anaileuita or Maronsita. A kabar word with a printed gloss is abekia. Thanks is surak or sura'.

## Safety Rules

- Do not invent vocabulary, ritual language, or kinship titles.
- Do not let one island stand for the kabupaten.
- Do not write adat, legal, or official text from a greeting name.
- Keep Indonesian when the Mentawai form is unknown.
- Ask for a sample when the user names a desa and wants local spelling.
- Do not mix Minangkabau into a Mentawai line.

## Practical Use

Use this skill for source-aware Mentawai openings and review of machine text. It fits chat and short counter notes. It does not fit a Mentawai-only announcement or a ceremony script.

## Registers

### Everyday

Attested kabar word from the Saguntung dictionary:

> abekia

Gloss: bagaimana; apa kabar. The errand after it stays in Indonesian. Mark the note mixed.

### Public

The district news note names Anaileuita and Maronsita as greetings to put on a public-service wall. Use one of those names, then Indonesian for the time and place. Do not add a translation the news note does not give. Mark the notice mixed.

### Professional

Admin text keeps deadlines, money, and document names in Indonesian. surak or sura' may close a short note as the glossed thanks word. Say which spelling you used.

### Adat boundary

Do not write uma ceremony lines, sikerei healing, punen, or wedding formulas. A dictionary gloss for sembuh on the word ale is not a healing script. Ask for the text the family already uses.
