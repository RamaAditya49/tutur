# Offline Brief

## Local Facts

- Purpose: ordinary messages, short greetings, and review for Bahasa Mentawai.
- Region: Kabupaten Kepulauan Mentawai, Sumatera Barat.
- Peta Bahasa sample villages: Monganpoula (Siberut Utara), Maileppet (Siberut Selatan), Sioban (Sipora), Makalo (Pagai Selatan).
- The 1978 Pusat Bahasa grammar studied dialek Sikakap as the variety the team treated as more widely used at that time.
- The 2025 Tuapejat launch said meaning and dialect can differ from island to island. The new district dictionary was meant as a shared word list, not as proof that one spelling fits every uma.
- ISO 639-3: `mwv`. Glottocode: `ment1249`.
- Source confidence: Level 2. Dictionary and grammar books exist. Worked glosses are the short ones copied in `local-mirror.md`.
- Speech habit: a public desk may put Anaileuita or Maronsita on the wall, then speak the errand in Indonesian. A kabar question with a dictionary gloss is abekia. Thanks is surak / sura'.
- Attested items are not a license to translate a paragraph.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent vocabulary, kinship titles, or ritual lines.
- Do not treat Siberut, Sipora, Sikakap, and Pagai as one dialect.
- Do not write uma ceremony, sikerei healing, punen, or wedding speeches.
- Keep Indonesian when the Mentawai word is missing.
- Do not borrow Minangkabau greetings and call them Mentawai.
- Do not equate Anaileuita with abekia. They come from different sources and the pemda note gives no gloss.
- Ask which island the reader is on before a public line.

## Sample Prompts

- "Use $tutur-mentawai to review this Bahasa Mentawai text."
- "Open a Tuapejat office note with Maronsita, then the requirement in Indonesian."
- "Ask after someone with abekia only, and keep the rest in Indonesian."
- "Check whether this spelling is surak or sura'."
- "Refuse a request for a healing or wedding formula."

## Safe Output Patterns

- Open a service note with Anaileuita or Maronsita as a named greeting, then Indonesian. Mark it mixed. Do not invent the translation.
- Use abekia when the meaning is "bagaimana" or "apa kabar".
- Use surak or sura' for thanks, and name the spelling choice.
- Quote "anai leuk ita" only as the dictionary's "ucapan selamat".
- For review, flag island mixing and unsourced ritual lines.
- For unknown terms, keep Indonesian and name the gap.
- For admin facts, keep time, price, and document names in Indonesian.
- Mark mixed lines as mixed.
