# Local Mirror

## Source Mirror

- Language: Bahasa Mentawai. The Saguntung dictionary also uses Nganga Mentawai for the language name in its older German-source title.
- ISO 639-3: `mwv`. Glottocode: `ment1249`. Glottolog alternative names include Mentawai and Mentawei. Status note: developing.
- Region: Kepulauan Mentawai. Peta Bahasa points sit on Siberut, Sipora, and Pagai Selatan.
- The 1978 grammar is dialek Sikakap. Do not spread its sentences, which are not copied here, onto every island.
- Balai Bahasa Sumatera Barat collected vocabulary in Pagai Utara and Pagai Selatan on 6–10 February 2021.
- Source confidence: Level 2.
- Glossed words below are from the Saguntung Mentawai–Indonesia–English dictionary.
- Anaileuita and Maronsita are names of greetings in the Dinas Kominfo Mentawai report of the 17 November 2025 launch. No Indonesian gloss is printed there.
- Source links are in `sources.md`. This file does not copy the dictionary.

## Local Usability

Use this file offline. A chat may use one glossed word and then switch to Indonesian. Island difference is the speech fact the bupati stated at the launch: the same utterance can carry a different sense from one island to the next. Say which island when you know it. Say "island unspecified" when you do not.

Minangkabau is the mainland neighbor. A line that "sounds Sumbar" may be Minangkabau. Do not answer a Mentawai request with it.

## Vocabulary And Semantics

Verified items only.

From the Saguntung dictionary:

- abekia: bagaimana; apa kabar; how; how are you.
- sura(k) or sura': terima kasih. The preface says the final sound is written k in some sources and as a hamzah in others. pusurakat is given as a noun built on that stem. Do not invent further derivatives.
- anai leuk ita: ucapan selamat. The dictionary gloss is selamat / congratulations, not a pinned "apa kabar".
- ale: (1) sembuh, pulih; (2) sapaan sesama laki-laki, semacam hai atau hallo. Use sense (2) only between men, and only as that short address. Sense (1) is a different meaning. Do not merge them into a healing formula.

From Dinas Kominfo Kabupaten Kepulauan Mentawai, 18 November 2025 report:

- Anaileuita: named as a sapaan for a public-service office wall.
- Maronsita: named beside it, same function.
- No gloss is given. Do not translate them beyond "sapaan".

Do not add sikerei lines, punen texts, wedding speeches, or kinship titles from travel articles.

## Generation Rules

1. Write the meaning in plain Indonesian first.
2. Add only Mentawai pieces listed above or supplied by the user with a source.
3. If a full sentence is not attested, keep it in Indonesian and label the message mixed.
4. Say the island when known. Otherwise say it is unspecified.
5. Do not treat anai leuk ita, Anaileuita, and abekia as one interchangeable sentence.
6. Do not borrow Minangkabau.
7. For public or professional text, keep time, place, price, and policy in Indonesian.
8. Refuse healing, wedding, and uma ceremony formulas.
