# Examples

## Target-Language Examples

- Indonesian: "Saya." Halus, lalu kasar.
  Bahasa Bima: "mada." "nahu."
  Note: Bahan Ajar Mulok Kelas IV Kota Bima, 2021, table nggahi halus / nggahi kasa. Hasan 2021: `mada` when the speaker is younger, `nahu` when the speaker is older or a peer. https://repository.uinmataram.ac.id/3277/1/Bahan%20Ajar%20Mulok%20Kelas%20IV.pdf and https://e-journal.my.id/onoma/article/download/1302/1167/
  Usage: Pick one for the relationship. Do not use both for the same speaker.
- Indonesian: "Kamu." Halus, lalu kasar.
  Bahasa Bima: "ita." "nggomi."
  Note: Same school table. Hasan: `ita` anda/tuan, halus. `nggomi` kamu to a younger person or a peer. `nggomi doho` kalian. `ita doho` anda sekalian, halus.
  Usage: One addressee, one set.
- Indonesian: "Sekolah di mana kamu, Achmad?"
  Bahasa Bima: "Sakola tabe nggomi Hama?"
  Note: Hasan 2021. Peer or younger addressee. `Hama` is his calling form for Achmad/Muhammad. Respectful nickname examples in the same paper, not a rule to reuse: Hama/Hima, Hasa/Heso, Mina/Mene.
  Usage: Quote it, or use it only with a peer the user has named this way.
- Indonesian: "Dia itu sangat rajin bekerja."
  Bahasa Bima: "Sia ede nawancuku cakana karawi."
  Note: Hasan 2021. `sia` is dia. His other glossed line: `Nggomi aina batu rawi sia doho aka` "Kamu jangan ikut-ikutan perbuatan mereka itu".
  Usage: These two sentences as published. Do not swap the verb.
- Indonesian: "Permisi / silakan." "Apa kabar?"
  Bahasa Bima: "Santabe ta." "Bune haba?"
  Note: Support phrase list, IDN Times NTB. `Santabe` "permisi" and `Bune` "bagaimana" also appear as separate entries on a community vocabulary page. The school book starts an unglossed sample with `Santabe ta la mada` and does not translate that whole sentence, so this skill does not either. https://ntb.idntimes.com/life/education/idn-times-hyperlocal/mengenal-bahasa-bima-dari-suku-mbojo-di-ntb
  Usage: Short openers only. `Haba tahopa` "kabar baik" is the same list’s reply, still support-level.

## Casual Chat

Input:

> Balas sepupu sebaya di Bima yang tanya kabar.

Output (mixed):

> Santabe ta. Bune haba? Nahu baik. Nggomi di rumah?

Note:

Peer set: `nahu`, `nggomi`. `Santabe ta` and `Bune haba?` are support phrases. "Baik" and the house question’s frame are Indonesian, so mark the line mixed. Do not add a made-up nickname.

## Public Reminder

Input:

> Ingatkan warga, usia campur, soal kerja bakti.

Output (mixed):

> Santabe ta. Besok kerja bakti jam 07.00 di balai. Bawa alat sendiri. Kabar ke ketua RT kalau berhalangan.

Note:

No `nggomi` toward the whole group. `ita` is not dropped into "mohon ita hadir" because that clause is not a glossed sentence. The body stays Indonesian.

## Professional Or Admin

Input:

> Admin kantor desa menulis ke orang yang lebih tua: berkas belum lengkap.

Output (mixed):

> Santabe ta. Mada kirim catatan ini. Berkas belum lengkap. Mohon kirim foto KTP sebelum jam 16.00.

Note:

`mada` fits a younger writer, from Hasan’s role note. The sentence "Mada kirim catatan ini" is mixed: `mada` is saya, and the verb phrase is Indonesian. It is not a full Nggahi Mbojo translation. Deadline and document name stay Indonesian. Do not use `nggomi` for this addressee.

## Adat Boundary

Input:

> Buat patu sambutan untuk tamu adat.

Output:

> Patu, kande, dan jiki tidak disusun di sini. Buku mulok Kota Bima menyebut genre itu, termasuk kande pada tuha ro lanti, sebagai tradisi yang sudah punya teks. Minta teks dari penutur. Sambutan sehari-hari yang bisa disusun: `Santabe ta.` Lalu nama tamu, jam, dan tempat dalam bahasa Indonesia.

## Review Mode

Input:

> Cek: "Nggomi ita, Bune haba, ayo ke Sumbawa pakai bahasa Samawa."

Response:

1. `nggomi` and `ita` are the kasa and halus words for kamu. They are not used together for one person.
2. `Bune haba?` is a support-level kabar question, not a travel sentence.
3. Bahasa Samawa is a different language. Peta Bahasa puts it about 81%–100% apart from Bima.

Safer rewrite (mixed):

> Santabe ta. Bune haba? Kalau yang diajak lebih tua, pakai `ita`. Rencana jalan dan jamnya ditulis dalam bahasa Indonesia. Bukan bahasa Samawa.
