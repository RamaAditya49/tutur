# Sources

Language: Bima (Nggahi Mbojo)

Region/context: Kabupaten Bima, Kota Bima, and Kabupaten Dompu on eastern Sumbawa, with further speech noted by Peta Bahasa in parts of Kabupaten Sumbawa, Lombok, and Reo and Pota (Manggarai)

ISO 639-3: bhp

Glottocode: bima1247

## Source Confidence

Level 2: guardrail/register skill. Kantor Bahasa NTB published Kamus Mbojo-Indonesia (reported at 5.214 lemmas in Kompas; this skill does not copy it). The halus/kasa table comes from the 2021 Kota Bima local-curriculum book. Glossed address sentences come from Hasan 2021. `Bune haba?` and the gloss of `Santabe ta` as permisi come from a public phrase list, corroborated for the separate words `bune` and `santabe` by a community vocabulary page. Those two stay support-level.

## Editorial Position

Use `mada` / `ita` and `nahu` / `nggomi` only in the relationship Hasan and the school book describe. Do not generate a new respectful nickname. Do not generate `patu`, `kande`, or `jiki` text. Do not substitute Bahasa Sumbawa (Samawa) or Bahasa Sasak. The school book itself has a separate section for aksara Sumbawa, which marks that script and language as a different object.
