# Tutur Bima

## Overview

Use this skill to produce, adapt, or review Bahasa Bima, also called Nggahi Mbojo, for ordinary messages. Peta Bahasa places the home area in Kabupaten Bima, Kota Bima, and Kabupaten Dompu, on the east of Sumbawa island, with further speech in parts of Kabupaten Sumbawa, Lombok, and Reo and Pota in Manggarai. ISO 639-3 `bhp`, Glottocode `bima1247`. Four dialects are named: Serasuba, Wawo, Kolo, and Kore. Dialectometry puts Bima about 81%–100% apart from Bahasa Sumbawa (Samawa) and Bahasa Sasak. Do not answer a Bima request with Samawa or Sasak.

The Kota Bima local-curriculum book calls the polite set `nggahi halus` and the plain set `nggahi kasa`. That split is the speech habit to protect. Pantun `patu`, `kande`, and sultanate song stay in review mode.

## Human Voice

Di Nggahi Mbojo, sopan atau tidaknya duduk di kata ganti: `mada` dan `ita` kalau yang bicara lebih muda, `nahu` dan `nggomi` kalau sebaya atau yang diajak lebih muda. `Santabe ta` dipakai untuk permisi. `Bune haba?` ada di daftar frasa publik untuk menanyakan kabar; jangan dirangkai jadi paragraf. Nama orang yang dihormati diubah bunyinya, tetapi polanya tidak tetap, jadi jangan karang sapaan baru. Ini bahasa timur Pulau Sumbawa, bukan bahasa Samawa dan bukan Sasak.

## Workflow

1. Identify the task.
   - Translation needs a glossed line from the school book, the Hasan 2021 address study, or a user sample.
   - Style adaptation picks halus or kasa from the relationship, then keeps the facts in Indonesian.
   - If the user asks for `patu`, `kande`, `jiki`, or sultanate speech, stop at review mode.

2. Check source confidence.
   - Read `references/sources.md` first.
   - Use Peta Bahasa to keep Bima apart from Samawa and Sasak.
   - Use the 2021 Kota Bima curriculum table for halus/kasa pairs. Use Hasan 2021 for who may say `nggomi` and for the glossed sample `Sakola tabe nggomi Hama?`.
   - Treat news and blog phrase lists as support for short greetings only.

3. Compose with source discipline.
   - Keep names, places, dates, and numbers unchanged.
   - Open with `Santabe ta` when asking permission, then the practical point.
   - Do not apply the published nickname pattern (Hama to Hima, Hasa to Heso, Mina to Mene) to a new personal name.
   - Label mixed Bima-Indonesian text as mixed.

4. Final pass.
   - Check halus and kasa are not mixed in one address to the same person.
   - Check the text is not Samawa or Sasak.
   - Do not claim native-speaker or official authority.

## Output Style

- For a direct request, return the message first.
- For a review, mark pronoun register and any invented name-address.
- For public or admin text, keep schedules, money, and documents in Indonesian.
- Mark mixed lines as mixed.
