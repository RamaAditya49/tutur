# Sources

Language: Sumbawa (Samawa, Semawa)

Region/context: western Pulau Sumbawa, Nusa Tenggara Barat. Peta Bahasa names dialek Sumbawa Besar as the standard, beside Taliwang, Jereweh, and Tongo, and separates the language from Sasak and Bima.

## Source Confidence

Level 2: guardrail and register skill. Kamus Samawa-Indonesia is a real dictionary from the NTB language office tradition, and published papers print short Sumbawa Besar lines with Indonesian glosses. Raise the level only after Taliwang, Jereweh, and Tongo examples are added from the same kind of source.

## Editorial Position

Ordinary Sumbawa Besar chat can reuse the transcript lines and the dictionary gloss of rungan. Public, adat, legal, and official wording needs a Samawa speaker of the named dialect.

The personal lesson page is support. It does not outrank the kamus or the papers. Sasak and Bima stay outside this skill.
