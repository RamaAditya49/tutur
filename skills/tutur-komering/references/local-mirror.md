# Local Mirror

## Source Mirror

- Language: Bahasa Komering. Catalog name Komering. Alternate name on Glottolog/Multitree: Kumoring. Also listed: Komerin, Njo.
- ISO 639-3: `kge`. Glottocode: `kome1238`.
- Family note: ABVD classifies it as Austronesian, Malayo-Polynesian, Lampung. Glottolog's subclassification comment points to Karl Anderbeck 2006. Related group is not the same language as Lampung Api (`ljp`) or Lampung Nyo.
- Badan Bahasa map: own language in Sumatra Selatan. Two dialects, Pulau Negara and Aji. Observation villages include Negeri Batin, Sriwangi (Semendawai Suku III), Campang Tiga (Cempaka), Sukaraja, Pulau Negara (Lubuk Linggau Timur), and Batu Raja Bungin, across OKU Timur, OKU, and OKU Selatan.
- Dialect gap on that map: about 51–80 percent between Pulau Negara and Aji. Gap from Pedamaran, Ogan, Melayu, Lematang, and Kayu Agung: about 81–100 percent.
- Phrase source: Kamus Komering Indonesia, https://kbdsumsel.kemdikbud.go.id/kki/kamus/words
- Source confidence: Level 2.
- The 2022 launch reported 4.479 lemmas and sublemmas covering both dialects. This mirror quotes only lines that were read. It does not copy the word list.

Source mirror notes:

- Komering on this map is not Bahasa Lampung and not Melayu Palembang.
- `tabik` in this dictionary is not the Lampung opener `tabik pun`.
- Examples are not tagged Pulau Negara or Aji on the page that was read.

## Local Usability

Use this mirror for a chat, a short reminder, or a review. Quote one printed example. Put names, hours, places, and money in Indonesian.

`gusti pohngun` is glossed saudara sekalian, para hadirin. The dictionary example is `api kabar gusti pohngun?` That fits a room of guests. It is stiff in a text to one cousin.

`nyak`, `niku`, and `sikam` recur inside examples as saya, kamu/engkau, and kami. They are safe inside the sentences printed below. They are not a template for new sentences.

## Vocabulary And Semantics

Short notes from the dictionary examples. The dash in the original entry stands for the headword.

- `tabik`: permisi kepada orang yang sedang duduk, sambil menundukkan badan. Example: `bakas sina tabik rik jolma tuha sina` (lelaki itu permisi kepada orang tua itu).
- `dang`: jangan. Example: `niku dang tidija ganta onti juga` (kamu jangan ke sini sekarang nanti saja). The dotted head `dang.kung` is glossed jangan dulu.
- `ai`: seruan. Example: `ai niku ja dang ngamuk juk sina` (wah kamu ini, jangan marah begitu).
- `amon`: kalau. Example: `amon hujan, nyak mak ratong` (kalau hari hujan, saya tidak datang).
- `ngapi`: mengapa. Example: `ngapi niku mak ratong bingi sina` (mengapa engkau tidak datang semalam).
- `api`: apa. Example shape: `api baka jambu sija` (apa wadah jambu ini).
- `dipa`: dimana. Example: `niku jona mojong dipa mak ku liak` (kamu tadi duduk dimana tidak saya lihat).
- `jakpa`: dari mana. Example: `jakpa niku, bingi-bingi ompai mulang?` (dari mana kamu, malam-malam baru pulang).
- `holau`: bagus. Example: `lambahanmu holau nihan` (rumahmu bagus sekali).
- `haga`: mau. Example: `nyak haga nginum` (saya mau minum).
- `ratong`: datang. Example: `tamuna kok ratong` (tamunya sudah datang).
- `tiuh`: kampung. Example shape in `tiuh sikam` (kampung kami).
- `gusti pohngun`: para hadirin. Example: `api kabar gusti pohngun?`
- `adat`: the example `sikam kahwin adat kumoring` means kami kawin adat komering. It is not a ritual text.

## Generation Rules

- Quote a line from the list above. Do not join two examples into a new sentence.
- Do not add Lampung particles, script, or clan formulas.
- Do not translate a whole announcement. One Komering line, then Indonesian.
- Label mixed text as mixed.
- If the user pastes a Komering sentence, check each word against this list. Unknown words stay flagged.
- Wedding, hiring-hiring, and pisaan stay with a speaker from the village named in the request.
- Keep the dictionary spelling of the quoted line, including `kumoring` inside `adat kumoring`.
