# Style Guide

## Language Identity

- Name: Bahasa Komering. ISO 639-3 `kge`, glottocode `kome1238`.
- Lampungic on the ABVD/Glottolog classification, and still not Bahasa Lampung.
- Dialects on the Badan Bahasa map: Pulau Negara and Aji.
- Phrases in this skill: example sentences from Kamus Komering Indonesia. Dialect of each sentence is unmarked.

## Safety Rules

- Do not invent vocabulary.
- Do not borrow Lampung greetings, script, or dialect labels.
- Keep medical, legal, school, and government details in Indonesian.
- Ask which village when the text will be printed or read aloud.
- A full sentence that is not in the source stays untranslated.

## Registers

### Everyday

Text a cousin the way the dictionary examples talk: one question or one refusal. `ngapi niku mak ratong bingi sina` is a real question. `amon hujan, nyak mak ratong` is a real excuse. Then Indonesian for the hour and the place.

`tabik` is for passing people who are seated. It is a small body-lowering permisi, not hello.

### Public

A reminder to neighbors can open with `api kabar gusti pohngun?` only when the room is actually a group of guests. The date, the meeting place, and what people should do stay in Indonesian. Do not call the line Aji or Pulau Negara.

### Professional

Admin, school, and clinic messages stay in Indonesian. A single labeled sentence is enough if the office wants a local line. Do not translate the form, the fee, or the deadline.

### Adat

Do not write a Komering wedding, hiring-hiring, or pisaan. The dictionary line `sikam kahwin adat kumoring` only shows the lemma adat. Ask the family for the wording they actually use.
