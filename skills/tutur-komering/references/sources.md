# Sources

Language: Komering

Region/context: Sumatra Selatan, especially Kabupaten Ogan Komering Ulu, Ogan Komering Ulu Timur, and Ogan Komering Ulu Selatan. Peta Bahasa names two dialects, Pulau Negara and Aji. Not Bahasa Lampung.

## Source Confidence

Level 2. The Balai Bahasa Sumatera Selatan dictionary is public as Kamus Komering Indonesia, and the lines in this skill were read from that word list. Glottolog and Peta Bahasa fix the catalog identity and the dialect map. The word list does not label each example as Pulau Negara or Aji, so this skill does not invent that split.

## Editorial Position

Ordinary chat may quote a dictionary example and carry the rest in Indonesian. Do not paste Lampung `tabik pun`, Api, or Nyo into a Komering line. Adat speech, including wedding wording, is out of scope even though the dictionary contains the short line `sikam kahwin adat kumoring`. That line is a usage example for the lemma adat, not a ceremony script. Hiring-hiring and pisaan are named in Balai Bahasa news as oral literature. This skill does not reconstruct them.
