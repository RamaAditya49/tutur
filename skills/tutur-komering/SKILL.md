# Tutur Komering

## Overview

Use this skill for ordinary Bahasa Komering in Sumatra Selatan. Peta Bahasa maps two dialects, Pulau Negara and Aji, at about 51–80 percent difference, and counts Komering as its own language against Pedamaran, Ogan, Melayu, Lematang, and Kayu Agung. Glottolog places it in the Lampungic group as ISO 639-3 `kge`, glottocode `kome1238`. That family link is not a license to copy Bahasa Lampung.

Source confidence: Level 2. Short lines below are example sentences from Kamus Komering Indonesia, Balai Bahasa Sumatera Selatan. The public word list does not mark which example is Pulau Negara and which is Aji.

Use this skill for source-aware Komering generation and review. It is not a certified translator. Public, school, legal, and adat text stays with a Komering speaker who knows the dialect.

## Human Voice

Tabik di kamus Komering bukan salam pembuka. Lema itu artinya permisi kepada orang yang sedang duduk, sambil menunduk, dan contohnya `bakas sina tabik rik jolma tuha sina`. Di contoh lain orang pertama `nyak`, orang kedua `niku`, dan larangan memakai `dang`, seperti `niku dang tidija ganta onti juga`. Jangan menukar pola itu dengan `tabik pun` atau dengan dialek Lampung Api dan Nyo. Kalimat yang tidak tercetak di kamus dibiarkan bahasa Indonesia.

## Workflow

1. Identify the task.
   - If the user names a place, keep Pulau Negara and Aji apart. Peta Bahasa puts Aji at Desa Negeri Batin, OKU Timur, and Pulau Negara at Sriwangi, Campang Tiga, Sukaraja, Pulau Negara, and Batu Raja Bungin.
   - If the place is unknown, do not assign a dialect to a dictionary line.

2. Check source confidence.
   - Read `references/sources.md` before adding a Komering word.
   - Quote a dictionary example. Do not build a new sentence by lining up single words.
   - Leave unknown words in Indonesian.

3. Compose with source discipline.
   - Keep names, places, dates, prices, and instructions unchanged.
   - Label mixed text as mixed.
   - `tabik` only when someone is asking to pass seated people. A cousin chat can open with a short attested line such as `ngapi niku mak ratong bingi sina` when that is actually the question.

4. Final pass.
   - Say the line comes from the Komering dictionary, not from a Lampung skill.
   - Do not claim a speaker in OKU Timur checked the wording.

## Output Style

- For a direct request, give the message first.
- For a review, point at Lampung wording and unsupported words, then give a shorter rewrite.
- For a public notice, keep the date, place, and instruction in Indonesian.
