# Examples

## Target-Language Examples

- Indonesian: "Iya."
  Bahasa Manggarai: "Iyo."
  Meaning/note: Wiktionary extract of Verheijen 1987 glosses Iya as Iyo. The page does not name the dialect.
  Usage: One-word acknowledgement. Do not attach new Manggarai grammar to it.
- Indonesian: "Baik."
  Bahasa Manggarai: "di'a."
  Meaning/note: Same wordlist glosses baik as di'a. Dialect unlabeled.
  Usage: Single-word answer. Not a full "saya baik" sentence.
- Indonesian: "Engkau."
  Bahasa Manggarai: "hau."
  Meaning/note: Same wordlist glosses engkau as hau. ité on that list is 'kita', a different pronoun.
  Usage: Cite the pronoun. Do not invent a polite second person.
- Indonesian: "Duduk melingkar."
  Bahasa Manggarai: "lonto leok."
  Meaning/note: klikhijau glosses lonto as duduk and leok as melingkar, and the phrase as duduk melingkar. The wordlist separately glosses lonto as duduk. Dialect unlabeled.
  Usage: Name the meeting practice. Do not script the meeting.
- Indonesian: "Tidak."
  Bahasa Manggarai: "toé."
  Meaning/note: Same Verheijen extract glosses tidak as toé. Dialect unlabeled.
  Usage: Single-word refusal or negation. The rest of a refusal stays in Indonesian.

## Casual Chat

Input:

> Balas saudara di Ruteng yang tanya kabar.

Output (mixed):

> Iyo. Aku baik. Sore ini aku di rumah, jam 4 boleh lewat kalau mau titip kunci.

Note:

Iyo is the only Manggarai word, and the wordlist does not say it is the Ruteng dialect. The rest is Indonesian. Mixed. di'a would only replace the word baik, still without a Manggarai sentence.

## Public Reminder

Input:

> Ingatkan warga kerja bakti besok pagi.

Output (mixed):

> Iyo. Kerja bakti besok jam 07.00 di kampung. Bawa sapu masing-masing. Kabar lagi kalau tidak bisa datang.

Note:

One attested word, then Indonesian. Mixed. Dialek tidak disebut di sumber kata. This is not a lonto leok script. For a notice in Lembor, Ruteng, or Manggarai Timur, ask a speaker of that village.

## Professional Or Admin

Input:

> Pesan admin puskesmas: jadwal imunisasi berubah.

Output (mixed):

> Iyo. Jadwal imunisasi berubah menjadi Jumat pukul 08.00 di puskesmas. Bawa buku KIA. Anak yang demam ditunda.

Note:

The time, place, and health instruction stay in Indonesian. ema and ende are kinship words, not titles for the nurse. Mixed. Dialect unlabeled.

## Adat Boundary

Input:

> Buat go'et baru untuk rapat tanah dan lonto leok.

Safe response:

> lonto leok di sumber artinya duduk melingkar. Go'et yang sudah tercetak, misalnya "Muku ca pu'u neka woléng curup, téu ca ambo néka woléng lako" (pisang satu rumpun jangan beda bahasa, tebu satu rumpun jangan beda jalan), dikutip apa adanya dan tidak dijadikan pola. Skill ini tidak menyusun go'et baru dan tidak mengisi urutan bicara lonto leok.

## Review Mode

Input:

> Cek teks ini: "Iyo, mari lonto leok, neka woleng curup versi baru, kita jaga hutan bersama."

Response shape:

1. Flag a new go'et shape.
2. Keep attested words that are only single glosses.
3. Ask for the village dialect before any public use.

Example review:

> Iyo dan lonto leok punya gloss di sumber: iya, dan duduk melingkar. "neka woleng curup versi baru" meniru potongan go'et yang sudah tercetak, lalu mengubahnya. Itu tidak dipakai. Tulis Iyo, lalu ajakan rapat dalam bahasa Indonesia, dan sebut dialeknya. Hasilnya mixed.
