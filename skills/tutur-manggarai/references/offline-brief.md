# Offline Brief

## Local Facts

- Purpose: ordinary messages, short words, and review for Bahasa Manggarai.
- Region: Flores, Kabupaten Manggarai Barat, Manggarai, and Manggarai Timur, Nusa Tenggara Timur.
- ISO 639-3: `mqy`. Glottocode: `mang1405`.
- Peta Bahasa dialects: Tangge (Desa Tangge, Lembor, Manggarai Barat), Manus, Rajong (Kesar), Kepo, and Rembong. The last four are mapped in Manggarai Timur in that entry. It also records Manggarai at Paka (Satar Mese) and Meler (Ruteng).
- Peta Bahasa separates Manggarai from Sikka, Lamaholot, and Komodo, with a difference of about 95–100%.
- Verheijen (1967) counted 43 subdialects in five groups: Barat, Barat-Tengah, Tengah, Timur, and Timur Jauh. A later West Manggarai study splits Kempo, Kolang (S to H), and a transition area.
- Source confidence: Level 2. Verheijen's Manggarai dictionary work is real. The words reused here come from a short published extract, not from a dialect-labeled field note.
- Speech habit: one Manggarai word, then Indonesian. Kinship words such as ema 'ayah' and ende 'ibu' are in the wordlist. lonto leok is the name of sitting in a circle to talk, not a sentence opener to invent around.
- The wordlist does not say which dialect it records. Repeat that limit.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent vocabulary, go'et, or adat formulas.
- Do not treat Ruteng, Kempo, Kolang, Tangge, or a Manggarai Timur village as the same speech.
- Do not write a lonto leok script, a wedding speech, or a land-dispute formula.
- Keep Indonesian when the Manggarai word is missing or the dialect is unnamed.
- Ask for the village before a public line.
- Do not mix Komodo, Sikka, Lamaholot, Ngada, or Rongga into a Manggarai line.
- Do not "correct" Kolang h into Kempo s, or the reverse, unless the user names the dialect.

## Sample Prompts

- "Use $tutur-manggarai to review this Bahasa Manggarai text."
- "Open a neighbor note with the attested Manggarai word for yes."
- "Check whether this go'et was copied from a source or newly composed."
- "Say which Peta Bahasa dialect is Tangge, and do not translate the notice into it."
- "Keep the clinic reminder in Indonesian and mark any Manggarai word as unlabeled for dialect."

## Safe Output Patterns

- Open with Iyo when a yes or an acknowledgement is needed, then give the errand in Indonesian.
- Use di'a only as the single word 'baik', not as a new sentence.
- For review, flag unlabeled dialect, neighboring Flores languages, and new go'et.
- For unknown terms, keep Indonesian and name the gap.
- For public use, ask a speaker of the village dialect.
- For admin facts, keep time, place, and money in Indonesian.
- Mark mixed lines as mixed.
