# Sources

Language: Manggarai

Region/context: Flores, Nusa Tenggara Timur, in Manggarai Barat, Manggarai, and Manggarai Timur. Peta Bahasa lists five dialects and separates the language from Sikka, Lamaholot, and Komodo.

## Source Confidence

Level 2: guardrail and register skill. Verheijen's Manggarai dictionary is a real dictionary, and the short wordlist used here is publicly attributed to his 1987 book. Dialect maps come from Peta Bahasa and from later dialect studies. Raise the level only after dialect-labeled sentences are added from a grammar or a speaker-reviewed sample.

## Editorial Position

Ordinary chat can use the short glossed words and must say that their dialect is unlabeled. Public, go'et, lonto leok, legal, and official wording needs a Manggarai speaker from the village in question.

Komodo, Sikka, and Lamaholot stay outside this skill. A new go'et is not a safe substitute for a missing sentence.
