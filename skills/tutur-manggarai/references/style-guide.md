# Style Guide

## Language Identity

- Name: Bahasa Manggarai.
- Folder: tutur-manggarai.
- ISO 639-3 `mqy`. Glottocode `mang1405`.
- Area: Flores, Manggarai Barat, Manggarai, and Manggarai Timur.
- Peta Bahasa dialects to name: Tangge, Manus, Rajong (Kesar), Kepo, Rembong.
- West Manggarai notes to name when relevant: Kempo, Kolang (S to H), and a transition area.
- Neighbors it is confused with: Bahasa Komodo, Bahasa Sikka, and Bahasa Lamaholot. Border villages may also use Bima or Ende, according to the Peta Bahasa note on Mangalili.
- Speech habit to name: a short word such as Iyo, then Indonesian. Dialect is the thing to ask, not a slogan about unity.

## Safety Rules

- Do not invent vocabulary, go'et, or adat formulas.
- Do not let one dialect stand for Manggarai.
- Do not write lonto leok, wedding, or land-dispute text from a gloss.
- Keep Indonesian when the Manggarai form is unknown or the dialect is unnamed.
- Ask for a sample when the user wants Kempo, Kolang, Tangge, or a Manggarai Timur variety.
- Do not mix those dialects, and do not mix in Komodo, Sikka, or Lamaholot.
- Quote a published go'et only as a quotation, with the journal gloss, and do not vary it.

## Practical Use

Use this skill for source-aware Manggarai openings, dialect warnings, and review of machine text. It fits chat and short community notes. It does not fit a Manggarai-only announcement or an adat speech.

## Registers

### Everyday

Attested one-word pieces, dialect unlabeled in the source list:

- Iyo. ('iya')
- di'a. ('baik')

The sentence after them is Indonesian. Mark the message mixed.

### Public

Use Iyo, then Indonesian for the time and place. Do not add lonto leok unless the user is naming that kind of meeting, and even then explain the term instead of scripting it.

> Iyo. Besok jam 07.00 kumpul di kampung. Bawa sapu sendiri.

That line is mixed. The Manggarai word is not tied to Tangge, Kempo, or Kolang in the source.

### Professional

For school, clinic, or village office, keep the deadline and the document in Indonesian. ema and ende are not job titles. A Manggarai hello is the single word Iyo, with the dialect gap stated.

### Adat

lonto leok means duduk melingkar in the published gloss. The go'et quoted in the local mirror is a fixed line from a journal. Do not write a new one, and do not turn lonto leok into a program of speeches.
