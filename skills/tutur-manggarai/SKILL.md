# Tutur Manggarai

## Overview

Use this skill for Bahasa Manggarai in Flores, Nusa Tenggara Timur. Peta Bahasa records it in Manggarai Barat, Manggarai, and Manggarai Timur, and lists five dialects: Tangge, Manus, Rajong (Kesar), Kepo, and Rembong. Older work counts many more subdialects. A word from an unlabeled list is not the speech of every village.

Short words are usable when a published list or a glossed article prints them. A full Manggarai paragraph is not. Komodo, Sikka, and Lamaholot are the languages Peta Bahasa separates from Manggarai.

Use this skill for source-aware Manggarai generation and review. It is not a certified translator. Dialect, go'et, adat, public, legal, educational, or official text stays with a Manggarai speaker of the named dialect when the line is not in the local sources.

## Human Voice

Iyo. Kalau mengabari tetangga, cukup iyo dulu, lalu jam dan tempat dalam bahasa Indonesia. Jangan menyusun go'et baru supaya pesan terasa adat. Peta Bahasa memisahkan Manggarai dari Sikka, Lamaholot, dan Komodo, jadi bahasa Flores yang lain tidak bisa ditempel di sini. Sebut dialeknya. Daftar kata yang dipakai skill ini tidak menamai dialek, dan di Manggarai Barat sumber membedakan Kempo, Kolang, dan daerah peralihan, termasuk bunyi s dan h.

## Workflow

1. Identify the task.
   - Translation needs a printed Manggarai sample from the target dialect.
   - Style requests keep the Indonesian meaning and add only attested Manggarai words.
   - Ask the village or dialect if the user says Manggarai without naming one.

2. Check source confidence.
   - Read `references/sources.md` first.
   - The everyday wordlist is the Wiktionary extract of Verheijen 1987. It has no dialect label. Say that every time those words are used.
   - Peta Bahasa and the West Manggarai dialect study are the dialect guides.
   - lonto leok is a named practice. Do not invent the speech that goes with it.

3. Compose with source discipline.
   - Keep names, places, titles, dates, and facts unchanged.
   - Use verified Manggarai words only.
   - If the sentence is not attested, write Indonesian and label the result mixed.

4. Final pass.
   - Name the dialect gap if the wordlist was used.
   - Do not claim native-speaker or official authority.
   - Do not answer in Komodo, Sikka, Lamaholot, Ngada, or Rongga.

## Output Style

- For a direct request, give the message first.
- For a review, name the risk, then give a safer rewrite.
- For public, adat, legal, school, or government text, ask for a Manggarai speaker of the named dialect.
- Label mixed Manggarai-Indonesian text as mixed.
