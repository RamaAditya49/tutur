# Offline Brief

## Local Facts

- Purpose: ordinary messages, reminders, and review for Bahasa Tidung.
- Region: Kalimantan Utara. Peta Bahasa names Bulungan, Malinau, Nunukan, and Tarakan, and the dialect write-up also names Tana Tidung villages.
- Dialects on Peta Bahasa: Berusu/Belusu, Sesayap, Tagul. Difference among them is given as about 52–61 percent.
- Speakers in Limbu Sedulun, Kujau, and Sesua call their speech Berusu or Belusu. Peta Bahasa still counts that isolek as Tidung, at about 67–68 percent difference from the other Tidung isolets.
- Sesayap villages include Salimbatu (Tanjung Palas Tengah) and Juata Laut (Tarakan Utara).
- Tagul is Desa Tagul, Kecamatan Sembakung, Kabupaten Nunukan.
- Catalog: Southern Tidung, ISO 639-3 `itd`, glottocode `sout3241`. Retired code `tid`. Northern Tidung is ISO `ntd`, glottocode `nort3262`, and is not this skill's phrase list.
- Phrase source: Salimbatu field study only. Level 1.
- This file is the offline substitute. It does not replace a dictionary.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent vocabulary, titles, or adat formulas.
- Do not write Kutai or Banjar and call it Tidung.
- Do not treat Sesayap, Berusu, and Tagul as one wording.
- Do not move a Salimbatu sentence into a Tarakan announcement without saying where it was recorded.
- Do not use Northern Tidung (`ntd`) words here.
- Keep schedules, money, health advice, and official instructions in Indonesian.
- If a full sentence is not in the source, write a short attested phrase and leave the rest in Indonesian. Label that text mixed.
- Possessive `-ku` is attested on nouns in the study (`seluarku`, `tinaiku`, `pensulotku`). Do not invent `-mu` or `-nya` from that pattern.

## Sample Prompts

- "Use $tutur-tidung to adapt this short chat for a cousin in Tarakan."
- "Review this text. Is it Bahasa Tidung or Banjar with a local label?"
- "Write a neighbor reminder. Sesayap only, Salimbatu phrases, rest in Indonesian."
- "Check whether these words are Berusu, Sesayap, or Tagul."

## Safe Output Patterns

- Open with one Salimbatu phrase, then the practical point in Indonesian.
- Name the dialect in the note when the audience is public.
- For an unknown word, keep the Indonesian word.
- For adat, ask for the wording used by the family or the adat elder. Do not compose a mantra.
