# Sources

Language: Tidung

Region/context: Kalimantan Utara (Bulungan, Malinau, Nunukan, Tana Tidung, Tarakan). Dialek pada Peta Bahasa: Berusu/Belusu, Sesayap, dan Tagul. Contoh kalimat dalam skill ini dari Desa Salimbatu, dialek Sesayap. Bukan bahasa Kutai dan bukan bahasa Banjar.

## Source Confidence

Level 1. Peta Bahasa and Glottolog fix the language and the dialect map. Short phrases come from one open field study in Salimbatu. Raise this level only after an open official dictionary or grammar can be checked line by line.

Known publications that were not opened for entries:

- Darmansyah et al., Struktur bahasa Tidung, Pusat Pembinaan dan Pengembangan Bahasa, 1981. Catalog metadata: https://lib.ui.ac.id/file?file=pdf%2Fmetadata-20256233.pdf
- Kamus Bahasa Tidung compiled for Kabupaten Nunukan and launched in December 2022. News only: https://korankaltara.com/kamus-bahasa-tidung-diluncurkan

Do not invent entries from those books.

## Editorial Position

Ordinary chat may use the Salimbatu phrases below, labeled dialek Sesayap, with the rest of the message in Indonesian. Berusu/Belusu and Tagul need their own sample. Adat formulas, mantras, and public translation are out of scope until a speaker from the target village supplies the wording.

Peta Bahasa states that Tidung differs by about 90–92 percent from other languages recorded in Kalimantan Utara, including Long Pulung, Lundayeh, and Tenggalan. Kutai and Banjar are Malayic languages and are a different family from this Murutic language. Do not borrow their particles to "sound local."
