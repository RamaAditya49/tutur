# Sources

Language: Paser (Pasir)

Region/context: Kabupaten Paser and Kabupaten Penajam Paser Utara, Kalimantan Timur. Ten Peta Bahasa dialects. Not Kutai.

## Source Confidence

Level 2: Kamus Bahasa Paser—Bahasa Indonesia is a real dictionary, published by Kantor Bahasa Provinsi Kalimantan Timur from work in Kabupaten Paser (field stages cited in the preface for 2007, 2008, 2009, and 2013, issued 2014). Phrases below are short examples from that dictionary. Dialect identity comes from Peta Bahasa. Glottolog lists Paser as `pasi1257` and does not assign an ISO 639-3 code. The kamus preface also records Andi Hasan's seventeen-dialect list in two groups, Olo Ot Danum and Olo Ot Ngaju. That older list is not the same labeling as the ten Peta Bahasa dialects, and this skill does not merge them.

## Editorial Position

Ordinary chat may repeat a glossed kamus phrase and must say the entry is not pinned to one of the ten dialects. Public and official logistics stay Indonesian. Soyong, the spoken request led by a mulung in the kamus preface, is not generated. Kutai pronouns and Kutai mendi do not belong in a Paser line.
