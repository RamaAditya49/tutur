# Local Mirror

## Source Mirror

- Language/register: Bahasa Paser (Pasir), everyday lines from the Kantor Bahasa dictionary.
- Region/context: Paser and Penajam Paser Utara, Kalimantan Timur.
- Glottocode: `pasi1257`. ISO 639-3: none assigned.
- Peta Bahasa idb=124. Ten dialects, listed in Local Facts of the offline brief.
- Dictionary: Kamus Bahasa Paser—Bahasa Indonesia. The preface says Darmansyah and colleagues described phonology, morphology, and syntax in 1979, and that this kamus continues Kantor Bahasa wordlist work. That 1979 book is not copied here.
- Older dialect grouping inside the preface: Olo Ot Danum and Olo Ot Ngaju, seventeen names. Keep it beside the ten-dialect Peta Bahasa list, not in place of it.

## Local Usability

Use this offline. A phrase is safe when the kamus prints it with a gloss. It is not safe as the voice of one village. Ask for Long Kali, Muara Toyu, Belengkong, Swan Slutung, Mentawir, or whichever dialect the listener uses.

## Vocabulary And Semantics

Kamus examples:

- `selamat ma` — selamat pagi. Also `selamat malom` selamat malam, `selamat nayap` selamat sore.
- `ise kabar iko?` — apa kabarmu? Same entry: `ise doli?` apa lagi?, `ise karanyo?` siapa namanya?
- `tabe depo ulun tuo` — permisi terhadap orang tua.
- `iko harus deo belajar basa Paser` — the kamus gloss reads "harus banyak belajar bahasa Paser". `iko` is the you of `ise kabar iko?` (apa kabarmu).
- `alamat iko mo mone?` — alamatmu di mana?
- `aku malan nowit okong` — saya jalan membawa adik.
- `man taka ngadap Bupati` — ayo menghadap Bupati.
- `nang derhaka depo ulun tuo!` — jangan durhaka terhadap orang tua.
- `nang ngako!` — jangan mencuri.
- `olo Ahad olo endo` — hari Minggu hari ini. The kamus prints the headword Ahad inside that frame.

Other glossed pieces: `depo` kepada or ke, `mo` di, `belo tau` tidak tahu (under `dene`, "dari dahulu aku tidak tahu"), `dero` mereka in `dero kakan malan-malan mo goa Lusan` (mereka mau jalan-jalan ke gua Lusan), `iyo` dia, `taka` kita in `derajat taka ene` "martabat kita ini". `ulun` orang. Do not build new sentences from these pieces.

## Generation Rules

1. Say the phrase is a kamus example and the dialect is unlabeled.
2. Match `selamat ma`, `selamat nayap`, or `selamat malom` to the time of day.
3. Use `iko` for you. Do not swap in Kutai `awak`.
4. Put addresses, hours, and money in Indonesian even if `alamat iko mo mone?` is the question frame.
5. Do not write soyong or a mulung script.
6. Do not mark the result as official or native-verified.
