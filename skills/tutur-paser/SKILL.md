# Tutur Paser

## Overview

Use this skill for Bahasa Pasir (Paser) in Kabupaten Paser and Kabupaten Penajam Paser Utara, Kalimantan Timur. Peta Bahasa counts ten dialects: Sepaku, Mentawir, Swan Slutung, Langon, Paser Belengkong, Sandeley, Sotek, Muara Toyu, Lomu, and Long Kali, with differences of 51%–79%. It is a language of its own next to Basap, Benuaq, Bulungan, and the other languages Peta Bahasa measures at 81%–100%.

It is not Bahasa Kutai. Kutai is Malayic, with Tenggarong `vkt` and Kota Bangun `mqg`. Paser has glottocode `pasi1257` and no ISO 639-3 code. The working sample is Kamus Bahasa Paser—Indonesia, Kantor Bahasa Provinsi Kalimantan Timur. The kamus discusses Kabupaten Paser and, in its preface, Andi Hasan's older seventeen-dialect grouping (Olo Ot Danum and Olo Ot Ngaju). Entries are not tagged to one of the ten Peta Bahasa dialects, so a kamus line is not automatically Long Kali or Mentawir.

## Human Voice

Pagi dibuka "selamat ma", sore "selamat nayap", malam "selamat malom". Kabar ditanya "ise kabar iko?". Izin ke orang tua "tabe depo ulun tuo". Kamu adalah iko, bukan awak-nya Kutai, dan mandi tidak dijadikan mendi. Kamus ini merekam Bahasa Paser Kabupaten Paser tanpa label desa pada tiap contoh, jadi Long Kali, Muara Komam, Belengkong, dan Mentawir tidak boleh disamakan. Soyong tidak disusun dari sapaan ini.

## Workflow

1. Identify the task.
   - Translation needs a printed Paser sample of the named dialect.
   - Style requests keep the Indonesian meaning and add only attested Paser phrases.
   - If the user names a village, say whether that village is one of the ten Peta Bahasa dialects. If not, ask.

2. Check source confidence.
   - Read `references/sources.md` first.
   - Phrases come from Kamus Bahasa Paser—Indonesia. They are short examples, not a copied page.
   - The kamus does not mark Sepaku versus Long Kali on each entry. Say that.

3. Compose with source discipline.
   - Keep names, places, titles, dates, and facts unchanged.
   - Use verified Paser phrases only.
   - If the sentence is not attested, write Indonesian and label the result mixed.

4. Final pass.
   - Do not claim native-speaker or official authority.
   - Do not answer in Kutai, Banjar, or Bajau Pondong.

## Output Style

- For a direct request, give the message first.
- For a review, name the risk, then give a safer rewrite.
- For public, adat, legal, school, or government text, ask for a Paser speaker of the named dialect.
- Label mixed Paser-Indonesian text as mixed.
