# Offline Brief

## Local Facts

- Purpose: short messages in Bahasa Melayu Ambon.
- What it is: a Malay contact variety and a lingua franca of Maluku, not an indigenous language of Ambon island.
- What it is not: bahasa tanah. Peta Bahasa records the indigenous cluster as Hitu, including Asilulu, Hitu, Laha, and related dialects.
- Codes: glottocode `ambo1250`, ISO 639-3 `abs`.
- Working dictionary: Kamus Dwibahasa Melayu Ambon-Indonesia, Balai Bahasa Provinsi Maluku, 2025.
- Pronouns in that dictionary and in APiCS: `beta` saya, `ose` and `ale` kamu, `katong` kita/kami, `dong` mereka.
- Possession in dictionary examples: `pung`, as in `beta pung bapa`.
- Other attested bits: `su` sudah, `jang` jangan, `seng` tidak, `par` untuk, `dangke` terima kasih, `da` selamat jalan.
- This skill should work without browsing.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent vocabulary or baileo ritual speech.
- Do not call Melayu Ambon a pure local language.
- Do not answer a bahasa tanah, Hitu, or Asilulu request with these Malay lines.
- Do not paste `beta` or `katong` onto an Indonesian sentence and call the result Melayu Ambon.
- `ose` and `ale` are both attested for you. APiCS notes `ale` as the intimate form. Do not add a third you-word.
- A full sentence must appear in the dictionary or the grammar notes in this skill.
- If the rest is not attested, continue in Indonesian and label the message mixed.
- `baileo` and `berang` occur in the dictionary as adat context. They are not a script.

## Sample Prompts

- "Use $tutur-ambon-malay to review this Melayu Ambon text."
- "Make a source-aware Melayu Ambon version of this note to a cousin."
- "Check whether this is Melayu Ambon or bahasa tanah."
- "Write a drain reminder with the dictionary sentence, then Indonesian for the rest."
- "Keep Melayu Ternate and Melayu Papua out of this reply."

## Safe Output Patterns

- Open with `Ale mo pi mana`, `Dangke`, or `Da` when that meaning matches.
- A neighbour warning can be the attested `Jang buang sampa di got`.
- A handwashing reminder can be the attested `ose` sentence from the dictionary.
- Put new times, prices, and places in Indonesian.
- For adat at a baileo, ask for the community's own text.
