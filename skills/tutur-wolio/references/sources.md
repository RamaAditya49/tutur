## Source Confidence

Level 2: guardrail/register skill. Kamus Wolio-Indonesia (Abas, Shaidy, and Jaya, Pusat Pembinaan dan Pengembangan Bahasa, 1985) is a real dictionary, and Peta Bahasa maps the dialects, including Wolio Keraton. Short glosses in this skill are taken from that dictionary's headwords and example lines. The PDF text layer interleaves columns, so only the lines that stay readable after that split are used. Court and sara language is review-only.

## Editorial Position

Everyday text may open with dictionary-backed `tabea`, `tulungi`, `inda`, and `ingkita`. Do not copy dictionary pages into an answer. Do not invent sultanate letters, sara formulas, or lines in Buri Wolio. The preface names written works such as Bulamaling, Indo Malussa, and Kaokabi, plus a fourth title the PDF text layer prints as "Kaluku xtnda". Naming them is not permission to compose new ones.

Peta Bahasa says Wolio in Baubau is spoken beside Muna, and Wolio in Buton beside Cia-Cia. A Muna `tabea` custom described in other papers is not this dictionary entry. Use the Wolio gloss: salam, permisi, maaf.
