# Offline Brief

## Local Facts

- Purpose: ordinary messages, adaptation, and review for Bahasa Wolio.
- Region: Kota Baubau, Kabupaten Buton, and Buton Selatan.
- ISO 639-3 `wlo`. Glottocode `woli1241`. Wotu-Wolio. Not Muna. Not Cia-Cia. Not Tolaki.
- Source confidence: Level 2, from Kamus Wolio-Indonesia (1985) and Peta Bahasa.
- Seven dialects in Peta Bahasa: Waruruma, Liabuku, Sorawolio, Wolio Keraton (Baadia, Murhum), Busoa, Pasar Wajo, and Kaimbulawa. Differences about 51%–78%.
- Dialectometry against Muna and Cia-Cia is about 81%–100%.
- The dictionary preface: Wolio is vocalic, every word ends in a vowel, and ordinary order is S-P or S-P-O.
- `tabea` in that dictionary means salam, permisi, maaf. The example line is `tabea kuloiopo`, glossed as maaf, saya lewat dahulu.
- `tulungi` means tolong. `inda` means tidak. `ingkita` is kita, and also a more respectful you.
- Keraton and sara language, plus texts named in the preface, are review-only.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent vocabulary, palace formulas, sara lines, or Buri Wolio spelling.
- Do not end a new Wolio word in a consonant.
- Do not replace Wolio with Muna because both are heard in Baubau.
- Do not use the Keraton dialect of Baadia for a market chat or a family text.
- `ingkita` as respectful you is attested. `ingkoo` is engkau. Do not invent another polite pronoun.
- Keep schedules, prices, permits, and identity data in Indonesian.
- Label mixed Wolio-Indonesian text as mixed.
- Public and official text needs a Wolio speaker.

## Sample Prompts

- "Use $tutur-wolio to review this Bahasa Wolio text."
- "Make a source-aware Wolio note for a cousin in Baubau. Not Muna."
- "Open a guest message with tabea and keep the room details in Indonesian."
- "Check that every Wolio word here ends in a vowel."
- "This text claims to be sara keraton. Review it. Do not add palace lines."

## Safe Output Patterns

- Open with `tabea`, then the point in Indonesian.
- Use `inda pokia` only for the dictionary sense tidak mengapa.
- Use `yapai ta porope ingkita?` only as the attested question ke mana anda menuju.
- Use `tulungi` as the verb tolong, not as a new sentence frame.
- For keraton, sultan, or Buri Wolio requests, return a review note and ask for a source line.
