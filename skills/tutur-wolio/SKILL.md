# Tutur Wolio

## Overview

Use this skill to produce, adapt, or review Bahasa Wolio for ordinary messages. Wolio is spoken in Kota Baubau, Kabupaten Buton, and Buton Selatan, Sulawesi Tenggara. ISO 639-3 `wlo`, Glottocode `woli1241`. Peta Bahasa records seven dialects, including Wolio Keraton in Kelurahan Baadia, Kecamatan Murhum. In Baubau it sits beside Bahasa Muna; in Buton it sits beside Cia-Cia. Dialectometry puts Wolio 81%–100% apart from both. Do not answer a Wolio request with Muna.

The 1985 Kamus Wolio-Indonesia says Wolio is a vocalic language: every Wolio word ends in a vowel, and ordinary clauses follow S-P or S-P-O. Court prose and sara/keraton formulas are named in that preface and in the Baadia dialect note. Do not invent them.

## Human Voice

Pesan Wolio yang pendek dibuka `tabea`, lalu langsung ke urusannya. Dari kamus 1985, `tulungi` adalah tolong, `inda` adalah tidak, dan `ingkita` dipakai kalau yang diajak perlu dijaga. Kata Wolio di kamus itu berakhir vokal; jangan tempel bunyi mati seperti kata Indonesia. Dialek Keraton di Baadia bukan bahasa chat pasar atau chat keluarga. Di Baubau bahasa ini hidup berdampingan dengan Muna, jadi jangan dicampur.

## Workflow

1. Identify the task.
   - Translation needs a dictionary example or a user sample.
   - Style adaptation keeps facts in Indonesian and adds only attested Wolio.
   - If the user asks for keraton, sultan, sara, or Buri Wolio literary text, stop at review mode.

2. Check source confidence.
   - Read `references/sources.md` first.
   - Use Peta Bahasa for where Wolio is spoken and which dialect is Keraton.
   - Use the 1985 dictionary glosses for short words. Do not copy dictionary pages into the answer.

3. Compose with source discipline.
   - Keep names, places, dates, and numbers unchanged.
   - Open with `tabea` when a greeting or excuse is needed.
   - Use `ingkita` for a respectful you, `ingkoo` for engkau. Do not invent a third polite pronoun.
   - Label mixed Wolio-Indonesian text as mixed.

4. Final pass.
   - Check that every Wolio word ends in a vowel.
   - Check that no Muna sentence has been substituted.
   - Do not claim native-speaker or palace authority.

## Output Style

- For a direct request, return the message first.
- For a review, separate everyday Wolio from Keraton/sara wording.
- For public or admin text, keep schedules, money, and identity documents in Indonesian.
- Mark mixed lines as mixed.
