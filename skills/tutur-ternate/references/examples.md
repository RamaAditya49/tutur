# Examples

## Target-Language Examples

- Target: `fangare ware ua`
  Indonesian gloss: Saya tidak tahu.
  Note: Watuseke, verb roots without extra affixes. `fangare` is the male polite I. https://openresearch-repository.anu.edu.au/server/api/core/bitstreams/0dcea0af-b156-401e-9671-5e1cb9e754c8/content
  Usage: Saying you do not know, in that polite male frame. Not a peer `ngori` sentence.

- Target: `ngana ware nage?`
  Indonesian gloss: Kamu tahu itu?
  Note: Same Watuseke section. `ngana` is the lower singular you.
  Usage: Only toward a younger or lower-rank listener, as Watuseke describes. Otherwise do not use this line.

- Target: `Ngori to-tego toma degodego`
  Indonesian gloss: Saya duduk di tempat duduk.
  Note: Duwila and Nurfani 2019, example (3), peer I for either gender in their account. https://www.researchgate.net/publication/334097475_PENANDA_KATA_GANTI_ORANG_DALAM_BAHASA_TERNATE
  Usage: A peer saying where they are sitting. Prefix `to-` stays on `tego`.

- Target: `Ngom mi-kudiho toma fala`
  Indonesian gloss: Kami pulang ke rumah.
  Note: Same article, example (4). `ngom` is exclusive we.
  Usage: Telling someone who is not in the group that the group is heading home.

- Target: `Ngana no-gulaha ake guraka`
  Indonesian gloss: Kamu membuat air jahe.
  Note: Same article, example (22). Prefix `no-` matches `ngana`.
  Usage: Comment on a younger listener making ginger water. Not a public address to elders.

- Target: `sidika`
  Indonesian gloss: Biarkan. Jangan diurusi.
  Note: Watuseke, imperative `si-` on `tika` "leave, abandon".
  Usage: A short "leave it" when that is the whole point.

## Casual Chat

Input:

> Kabari saudara bahwa kami sudah pulang ke rumah dan sampai kira-kira jam tujuh.

Output (mixed):

> Ngom mi-kudiho toma fala. Perkiraannya jam tujuh.

Why:

The Ternate clause is the exclusive-we line from Gramatika. The hour has no attested Ternate sentence, so it stays Indonesian.

## Public Reminder

Input:

> Umumkan ke semua usia supaya kumpul di balai jam 4.

Output:

> Kumpul di balai jam 16.00. Pengumuman untuk segala usia tidak dibuka dengan ngana, karena Watuseke membatasi ngana pada lawan bicara yang lebih muda atau lebih rendah.

Why:

No attested clause addresses a mixed-age public. The reminder stays Indonesian and the pronoun limit is stated.

## Professional Or Admin

Input:

> Konfirmasi ke atasan bahwa berkas cuti belum diketahui hasilnya.

Output (mixed):

> Fangare ware ua. Berkas cuti belum ada kabar. Tanggal yang diajukan tetap 12 sampai 14.

Why:

`fangare ware ua` is the attested "I don't know", and it uses the polite male I. The leave dates stay Indonesian. If the speaker is not male, do not swap in `fajaru` unless the user confirms that frame, because this exact not-knowing sentence is cited with `fangare`.

## Adat Boundary

Input:

> Buat sambutan di depan kesultanan.

Safe response:

> Sambutan kesultanan tidak dibuat di sini. Duwila menyebut bahasa kolano sebagai latar sejarah, bukan sebagai contoh kalimat. Skill ini hanya memegang klausa harian seperti duduk, pulang, dan tidak tahu.

## Review Mode

Input:

> Cek: "Ngana tar mau pi di pante, torang tunggu."

Response:

1. `tar`, `pi`, and `torang` are Melayu Ternate, as in the wisatawan phrase list that glosses `ngana tar mau pi di pante` as Malay. They are not Bahasa Ternate clauses.
2. Bahasa Ternate would need a prefix pair such as `ngana no-`, and only inside a sentence the sources actually give, for example `Ngana no-gulaha ake guraka`.
3. That ginger-water sentence does not mean "you do not want to go to the beach", so it cannot replace the Malay line. Safer reply: `Saya tunggu kabarnya. Kalau mau bahasa Ternate, kirim contoh dari penutur untuk kalimat pantai itu.`
