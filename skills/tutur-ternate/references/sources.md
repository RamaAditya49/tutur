# Sources

Language: Bahasa Ternate (North Halmahera)

Region/context: Pulau Ternate and Ternate communities in Maluku Utara, including the west coast of Halmahera in the Glottolog and Peta Bahasa descriptions. Not Tidore. Not Melayu Ternate.

## Source Confidence

Level 2. Watuseke's grammatical sketch and the Kantor Bahasa Maluku Utara article give full short sentences. Atjo 2008 is a dictionary cited by that article; this skill does not copy the dictionary.

## Editorial Position

Write Bahasa Ternate only as attested clauses with the matching prefix. When Peta Bahasa groups a Tidore observation point under Ternate, say so, then keep Tidore out of the generated text because Glottolog and the Tidore grammar tradition treat it as its own language. Melayu Ternate examples are listed so they can be recognised and refused. No sultanate formula is generated. The historical label bahasa kolano in Duwila's introduction is background, not a script.
